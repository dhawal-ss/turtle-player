## Mechanics notes

AUDIOBOOK HOUR METERING (support /us/article/audiobooks-premium-plans): the included amount "varies per country and renews automatically each billing cycle" — US page 15h, Iceland-English page 12h, 2026 expansion markets 12h. Premium Individual plus Duo/Family PLAN MANAGERS only (manager's own account); Student excluded. Time is counted at normal speed, so 2x playback does not stretch the allowance. Re-listening meters again. Unused hours expire at renewal; no refunds. Top-ups are one-time, valid 12 months, lost if the subscription lapses. CHAPTERS: manual chapters are parsed from the episode description — "(00:00) Intro", MM:SS or HH:MM:SS, first at 00:00, chronological, titles under 40 chars, at least 3 chapters, at least 30s between start times; manual overrides automatic. External hosts can send Podlove chapters in RSS. Automatic chapters come from a generated transcript with Spotify choosing titles and start points; English only, not all shows. TRANSCRIPTS: uploads must be VTT or SRT, max 5MB, timestamps required or it "won't sync during playback"; turning on auto-generated transcripts replaces an uploaded one. Spotify pushes transcripts OUT to RSS; ingesting them from RSS is not described anywhere on the page. PARTNER PROGRAM GATING: host on Spotify for Creators; legal address in an eligible market; at least 3 published episodes; at least 2,000 consumption hours on Spotify in the last 30 days; at least 1,000 audience count in the last 30 days. A "stream" = 60+ seconds, and streams (not plays) drive eligibility and revenue. Staying eligible needs one episode plus at least $10 earned per 6 months. Ads pay a 50% revenue share. Premium video pay uses a "proprietary payment formula"; factors may include qualifying consumption time, users' registration market and the number of Premium users with ad-free video access; adjustable at will. GENERATIVE RETRIEVAL (Jan 2026): catalog discretized into Semantic ID token sequences; beam search over the LLM output space emits candidate sequences decoded back to catalog items; candidates re-ranked by model log-probabilities via multi-item scoring with candidate masking. Personalization: a user embedding projected into the LLM embedding space as a single soft token inserted before the first forward pass, with partial fine-tuning; raw user text used sparingly for latency. Cadences: podcast charts daily, audiobook charts weekly, prompted playlists daily/weekly/never with a per-episode reason.

## Spotify weaknesses

- **No skip silence and no custom skip interval** — Spotify's own podcast support page documents only three playback controls: a 1x speed control, 15-second skip forward, 15-second rewind, and download. No silence trimming, no adjustable skip interval, no per-show speed memory and no podcast sleep timer appear on any Spotify page fetched. The only silence-trim evidence is a 2020 code sighting that never shipped. (https://support.spotify.com/us/article/podcasts-and-shows/)
- **Podcasts and audiobooks cannot be excluded from the taste profile** — "You can only exclude playlists or tracks from your taste profile." There is no documented way to keep a show, an episode or an audiobook out of personalization, so a single curious spoken-word session permanently feeds the profile that drives Home. (https://support.spotify.com/us/article/exclude-playlists-or-tracks-from-your-taste-profile/)
- **Spoken word is not kept separate from music personalization** — Taste Profile is one profile spanning music, podcasts and audiobooks, and Spotify documents cross-domain transfer: "If a podcast is popular with listeners of your favorite musician, we might recommend that podcast to you." The 2T-HGNN paper likewise uses podcast and music preferences to recommend audiobooks. Nothing offers a spoken-word boundary. (https://newsroom.spotify.com/2026-09-23/taste-profile-shape-home-feed/)
- **Taste Profile steering is Premium, 18+, two markets** — The only documented way to tell Spotify what you want less of in Home is Taste Profile (Beta), limited to Premium listeners aged 18 and over in the US and New Zealand. Everyone else has no stated lever beyond excluding music tracks and playlists. (https://newsroom.spotify.com/2026-09-23/taste-profile-shape-home-feed/)
- **Audiobook hours expire monthly, re-listening is metered again, top-ups are forfeited** — Unused hours do not carry over and "We don't offer refunds for unused or expired audiobook listening time." Re-listening to a passage deducts again, so re-reading a favourite chapter costs the allowance twice. Top-ups are valid 12 months but access is lost if the subscription lapses. Falling asleep mid-chapter burns metered hours (inferred). (https://support.spotify.com/us/article/audiobooks-premium-plans/)
- **Included audiobook hours exclude Students and all Duo/Family members** — "The Premium Student plan doesn't include audiobook listening time." On Duo and Family only the plan MANAGER receives hours, and only for their own account; members must buy their own. Paying into the same household plan gives most members nothing. (https://support.spotify.com/us/article/audiobooks-premium-plans/)
- **Audiobook entitlement and catalog differ sharply by market** — The amount "varies per country": 15 hours on the US page, 12 on the Iceland-English page, 12 in the 2026 expansion markets; Audiobooks+ is 15 hours at $11.99 in the US but 20 hours at EUR 9.99 in Iceland. New markets get 350,000+ titles against 700,000+ in the original 22, and across much of Africa and Asia there are no included hours at all. (https://newsroom.spotify.com/2026-10-07/audiobooks-global-expansion/)
- **No manual bookmarks, and chapters cannot be downloaded individually** — The audiobook help article documents speed, a sleep timer, 15-second skips, samples, ratings and whole-book download, but no manual bookmark or note feature, and states "You can't download individual chapters." Progress saving is mentioned only as recovering your place when hours run out. (https://support.spotify.com/uk/article/audiobooks/)
- **Transcripts are mobile-only and partial; automatic chapters are English-only** — "Listeners see transcripts in the Now Playing View and on episode pages in the Spotify mobile app" — not desktop, web or connected devices. "Some shows have auto-generated episode transcripts", with eligibility undefined, and "Transcripts aren't available to all creators." Automatic chapters "are currently only generated in English, and they aren't available to all shows yet." (https://support.spotify.com/us/creators/article/transcripts/)
- **Skip ahead is a foreground mobile test creators cannot opt out of** — It reaches only "some Spotify Premium users in select markets", runs on iOS and Android in the Now Playing View and is "not available on desktop, web, or connected devices." It applies "regardless of content or publisher" and "can not be opted out at the show or organization level." Podnews adds that Spotify pays third-party podcasters nothing while skipping their sponsor reads. (https://support.spotify.com/us/creators/article/understanding-skip-ahead-button/)
- **Ad-reduced video podcasts only exist for participating shows in Partner Program markets** — Premium's uninterrupted video viewing is tied to the Partner Program, so it applies only to enrolled shows in eligible markets; elsewhere, and for shows below the 2,000-hour / 1,000-audience bar, Premium subscribers still get dynamic ads. Baked-in sponsorships always remain. (https://newsroom.spotify.com/2026-09-17/partner-program-expansion-global/)
- **The AI discovery features are Premium-only, English-only and in a handful of markets** — Prompted Playlists for podcasts are beta, Premium, English only, in roughly six markets. In-episode AI Q&A launched to "Premium mobile users in the U.S., Sweden, and Ireland" only. A listener outside those markets, or on the free tier, gets none of Spotify's 2026 podcast discovery work. (https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/21/spotify-adds-ai-powered-qa-and-briefing-generation-features-to-podcasts/)

## Unverifiable

- The actual podcast playback speed values and range. Community posts say up to 3.5x, but no Spotify page fetched lists the options for podcasts or audiobooks.
- Whether any sleep timer exists for PODCASTS. It is documented only on the audiobooks help page; the podcasts-and-shows page does not mention one.
- Whether transcripts highlight words in sync as they are spoken. The fetched support article only says timestamps are required or the transcript "won't sync during playback".
- Whether Spotify ingests transcripts from a show's RSS feed. RSS.com reports it does not; Spotify's page only describes pushing transcripts OUT to RSS.
- What's New feed limits (search summaries claim up to 100 episodes retained for 60 days). Not present on either podcasts-and-shows page fetched.
- Whether per-show new-episode push notifications exist via a bell icon. Not described on the support pages fetched; only a link to a separate notification-settings article.
- Whether in-episode AI Q&A is grounded in the episode transcript. Press reports say so; no Spotify-owned page confirms the mechanism.
- Whether Spotify-sold ads are exempt from Skip ahead. The support page says it applies "regardless of content or publisher"; press conflicts, and Spotify states it "does not make podcast ads unskippable".
- Whether podcast or audiobook listening measurably shifts MUSIC recommendations (Discover Weekly, Release Radar, Wrapped). Spotify documents the reverse direction only.
- Whether audiobook progress syncs across devices as a named feature, whether automatic bookmarking exists, and whether video podcasts support a speed control at all.
