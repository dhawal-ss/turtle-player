## Mechanics notes

CHARTS (support.spotify.com/.../understanding-spotify-charts): daily charting day 00:00-23:59 UTC, usually published before 6PM EST (22:00 UTC) the next day; weekly week runs Fri 00:00 to Thu 23:59 UTC, published after it ends globally. Eligible = title live before or during that window. Chart counts come from an undisclosed filtering formula; Spotify states not every stream or track is chart-eligible, so chart numbers differ from in-app and Spotify-for-Artists counts, and filtering does not affect royalties. Streak = consecutive days/weeks charted, resets on re-entry; peak never resets. A stream counts at 30s of play (how-we-count-streams); whether charts apply the same bar is not stated. Local Pulse = songs "uniquely popular" in a city relative to overall popularity, i.e. a local-vs-global lift ratio (inference; formula unpublished). Artist charts = 200 artists by catalogue-wide streams; genre charts assign songs from user-playlist context plus editorial feedback (2021 blog). PODCAST SEARCH (engineering.atspotify.com 2022-03): siamese bi-encoder on multilingual USE-CMLM; item text = episode plus show title and description. Training pairs: log successes; a failed query paired with the episode its reworded retry won (low word overlap); BART/MS-MARCO synthetic queries. In-batch negatives (B positives, B^2-B negatives), later hard-negative mining plus margin loss. Episode vectors precomputed and indexed in Vespa, ANN over tens of millions of episodes, Vespa first-phase rerank on popularity signals, GPU query encoder with a query-vector cache, top 30 returned. It is an extra retrieval source beside Elasticsearch because dense retrieval loses to term matching on exact-match queries. QUERY GATING (research.atspotify.com 2021-03): queries classified focused vs non-focused from standalone, reference-dependent (embedding and pronunciation distance to gold broad queries) and interaction (click entropy) features; long-tail items are injected only on non-focused queries, trading exposure gain against satisfaction loss. RESEARCH, NOT SHOWN SHIPPED: Neural Instant Search (per-keystroke, char-level prefix embeddings, intent-type multi-task head); Semantic-ID LLM "comparable to production" in search; agentic LLM router (+115% similar artists, +25% broad music). UPCOMING RELEASES: personalised from listening history plus a global top 10 of Countdown Pages by pre-saves, refreshed daily; Countdown Chart posts that top 10 weekly on Wednesdays.

## Spotify weaknesses

- **Viral charts retired with no algorithmic replacement** — Spotify's Viral 50 charts (global and country) disappeared around 14 May 2026. A spokesperson said Spotify "retired its viral charts as part of an ongoing effort to focus on features that best reflect how listeners engage with music today"; the reported motive was botted and low-effort tracks. The only substitute is the editorial Viral Hits playlist, so no algorithmic momentum chart remains. (https://musically.com/2026/05/18/spotify-retires-viral-charts-but-viral-hits-playlist-lives-on/)
- **Chart eligibility is an undisclosed black box** — Spotify says chart numbers come from a formula that "protects the integrity of our charts", that "not every stream or track is chart-eligible", and that chart counts differ from the counts in the app and in Spotify for Artists. It never says which streams are dropped or what threshold applies, so chart positions cannot be reproduced or audited outside Spotify. (https://support.spotify.com/us/artists/article/understanding-spotify-charts/)
- **The most granular charts are web-only and login-gated** — City charts are "only available at charts.spotify.com", not in the app. Genre, City and Local Pulse charts, plus the full Top 200 and the streak/peak/entry-date detail, all require signing in. The most interesting discovery surface Spotify builds from its own data is the one a listener browsing the app cannot reach. (https://support.spotify.com/us/artists/article/understanding-spotify-charts/)
- **Genre is assigned to you, with no correction path** — Genre charts categorise songs "using context from user playlists and editorial feedback". Artists cannot edit a song's genre; they can only flag a miscategorisation to support. Listeners get no way to see or dispute why a track sits in a genre, and genre hubs inherit whatever that pipeline decided. (https://artists.spotify.com/en/blog/celebrating-artist-success-with-spotify-charts)
- **The genre map was abandoned: Every Noise at Once froze in 2023** — Every Noise at Once, the browsable map of Spotify's thousands of micro-genres, stopped updating after its creator was laid off in December 2023 and lost access to internal data. Spotify reportedly said that status was likely to persist for the foreseeable future. Its data still powers "Fans also like", but the only surface for navigating genre space exhaustively is frozen. (https://techcrunch.com/2024/02/12/every-noise-shut-down-spotify-layoffs)
- **Search operators cover music only, and are undocumented per platform** — All seven documented operators (year, genre, label, track, album, artist, tag) target music. There is no operator for podcasts, episodes, audiobooks, playlists or profiles, none for duration, BPM, key or explicit, no year ranges, and tag:new has one fixed two-week window. The page never says which platforms honour the operators. (https://support.spotify.com/us/article/Advanced-search1/)
- **Voice search was removed for everyone, then returned only for a few** — The in-app "Hey Spotify" voice feature was deprecated on 18 July 2022, with the search-bar mic icon removed, and Spotify directed users to third-party assistants instead. The conversational replacement (Talk to Spotify, July 2026) is Premium-only, 18+, English-only and limited to five markets, so most listeners have had no first-party voice search for over four years. (https://community.spotify.com/forum/en/discussion/5411511/hey-spotify-disappeared-from-iphone-app)
- **Lyrics search needs an exact, ordered phrase of three or more words** — The help page requires at least three words of lyrics, and reported behaviour is that only results tagged "Lyrics match" contain the exact phrase in the typed order. A misremembered line, a paraphrase or a two-word hook fails, and the "Lyrics match" tag is reported not to appear on desktop, so the user cannot tell which hits actually matched lyrics. (https://nerdschalk.com/how-to-search-songs-by-lyrics-on-spotify/)
- **Recent searches are unreliable and not really deletable** — Users report recent searches disappearing on their own every couple of days, so the list is not dependable as a history. Clearing it removes the displayed entries only, with no guarantee that stored search records are deleted, and there is no documented control over search-history retention. (https://community.spotify.com/t5/Social-Random/How-do-you-clear-suggested-search-history/td-p/6027593)
- **No way to exclude content types you never want** — Listeners asking to hide or filter out audiobooks in search results are told there is no way to permanently exclude them from results or search history. Filtering is per-query via tabs; there is no persistent preference that keeps audiobooks, podcasts or other formats out of the default result set. (https://community.spotify.com/t5/Live-Ideas/Option-to-disable-filter-Audiobooks-in-Search-Results/idi-p/7351449)
- **Search result coverage has been visibly broken in 2026** — Spotify's own February 2026 Ongoing Issues review lists "search results severely limited" (a report of only 27 results for the query "love") as an open issue needing more information, alongside a separate search bug where the keyboard failed to appear, fixed in app version 9.1.20. Basic retrieval breadth is not a solved problem in the shipped client. (https://community.spotify.com/t5/Community-Blog/Ongoing-Issues-Review-February-2026/ba-p/7358392)
- **Browse is fragmented across platforms** — The Upcoming Releases hub launched as a Search-tab tile on mobile, with no stated desktop parity. On desktop, Browse was folded into Search and users reported genre and mood categories opening to empty lists for months; a related thread reports Spotify's API no longer returning Browse, which cut off genre browsing in Sonos. (https://community.spotify.com/t5/Desktop-Mac/Where-is-Browse-section-on-Mac-desktop-app-Oct-2022/m-p/5441373)

## Unverifiable

- Spotify's typo tolerance specifics: edit distance, auto-correct versus suggestion, and whether a 'showing results for' affordance exists. Only an engineer interview confirms spell correction exists; no Spotify-owned page documents behaviour.
- Chart movement indicators beyond streak, re-entry and peak. Up/down arrows, position deltas and a NEW badge are widely described by third parties but no Spotify-owned page documents them.
- How many markets have country charts today. A Kaggle community dataset is titled '73 countries'; that is a third-party project. The last Spotify-stated figure is 'top 65 markets' for artist charts in 2021.
- Whether the 2021 figures still hold in 2026: 17 genre charts, top 65 markets, 200+ cities, Top 50 free versus Top 200 with login. The current help article names only city charts and Local Pulse and states no sizes.
- Whether Neural Instant Search, the Semantic-ID LLM search model, or the agentic LLM query-understanding router serve listener traffic. Only the 2022 podcast NLS post claims rollout; the others report offline or A/B results.
- The current exact 'Browse all' category list and labels. No Spotify-owned page enumerates them; evidence is community and press reporting, and the layout has changed over time.
- Whether Spotify formally announced the Viral chart retirement or removed it silently. Coverage conflicts: one account says no formal announcement, another cites a spokesperson statement to Billboard.
- Whether chart-eligible streams use the same 30-second threshold as royalty-counted streams. The 30s rule is documented for stream counting; chart filtering is documented only as an unspecified formula on top of it.
- The free tier's on-demand time allowance and skip caps after it is exhausted. The 2025-09-15 newsroom post states no limits; the circulating numbers come from press.
- Whether search indexes podcast transcripts or audiobook text. The 2022 NLS post lists episode title, description, show title and description as indexed fields; no source states transcript or book-text indexing.
