## Mechanics notes

PARITY RULE: "Spotify Premium" is not one product; any comparison must name the tier AND the market. All figures fetched 2026-11-24. TWO TIER ARCHITECTURES. (A) US, GB, BR, PH, NG: Individual/Duo/Family/Student, with lossless and the AI features (where present at all) inside base Premium. (B) IN, ID, ZA, AE: Premium Standard (~320 kbps; no lossless, DJ, AI Playlist, Mix or DJ-software link) vs Premium Platinum at ~2.6x the price, up to 3 accounts at one address, adding lossless, Mix, AI DJ, AI playlist creation, DJ software and (ZA, AE) plan-manager audiobook hours. QUALITY LADDER (one article, both tiers): Automatic, Low ~24, Normal ~96, High ~160 kbit/s on Free and Premium; Very high ~320 and Lossless (24-bit/44.1 kHz FLAC) Premium only. Web player is fixed: AAC 128 Free, 256 Premium. Podcasts ~96 kbit/s. Lossless needs mobile >=9.0.58 or desktop >=1.2.67 and 1.5-2 Mbps, excludes music videos, podcasts and audiobooks, and on third-party gear must be set in Spotify Connect device settings; Bluetooth/AUX may not carry it. DOWNLOADS: Premium only (Free gets podcasts), 10,000 tracks on each of up to 5 devices, albums/playlists/podcasts only (single songs must be wrapped in a playlist), expiring unless the app goes online once every 30 days. FREE IS ON-DEMAND NOW. Since 2025-09-15, globally, Free can search a track and play it instantly. What Free still documentably lacks: the mobile queue (For: Premium), shuffle styles, hosting or remotely joining a Jam, lossless, downloads, Mix transitions, music videos, DJ, prompted playlists. Smart Shuffle is always active for Free on mobile. AI IS ENGLISH-FIRST, BETA, METERED. DJ: 75+ markets, 6 languages (EN, ES, FR, DE, IT, pt-BR), 9 voices, voice or typed requests, DJ button reshuffles genre/artist/vibe, commentary can be switched off (2026-11-10). AI Playlist: 47 markets, English. Prompted Playlist: Premium mobile, English, "test markets", daily/weekly auto-refresh, per-track "why it fits" notes, Refine vs Edit prompt; a shared prompt regenerates for Premium but Free only hears the frozen list. Studio: macOS-only app, Premium 18+. Talk to Spotify: Premium 18+. All carry undisclosed usage limits; overflow sells as Pro credits, valid 12 months, no rollover, included allowance spent first. AUDIOBOOKS: amount varies per country (15 h US/GB, 12 h BR, none listed on IN/ID/PH/NG plan pages), plan-manager-only on Duo and Family, none on Student, unused hours expire monthly.

## Spotify weaknesses

- **"Premium" means materially different things in different markets** — In India, Indonesia, South Africa and the UAE, base Premium Standard has no lossless, no AI DJ, no AI playlist creation, no Mix and no DJ-software link; all sit behind Platinum at roughly 2.6x the price. A listener on "Premium" in Mumbai gets strictly less than one on "Premium" in London, with no in-product signal that the features exist elsewhere. (https://www.spotify.com/in/premium/)
- **Lossless stops at 44.1 kHz and is still missing from whole storefronts** — Spotify documents lossless only as 24-bit/44.1 kHz FLAC: no higher sample rates, no hi-res, no spatial or immersive format. The Brazil, Philippines and Nigeria plan pages advertise no lossless at all, only "~320 kbps", more than a year after the September 2025 launch. (https://www.spotify.com/br/premium/)
- **Lossless does not reach spoken audio or many real connections** — "Lossless is unavailable on Music Videos, Podcasts or Audiobooks." Podcasts stay at about 96 kbit/s in apps. On third-party gear lossless has to be selected inside Spotify Connect device settings, and Spotify warns that Bluetooth or AUX "may not support lossless quality", so the headline feature often silently does not apply. (https://support.spotify.com/us/article/lossless-audio-quality/)
- **Mix and transitions die over Spotify Connect** — Mixed playlists play as regular playlists over Spotify Connect, and crossfade, automix and gapless "can't be set when using Spotify Connect". The flagship 2026 Premium mixing feature degrades on exactly the speakers and receivers people own, with no warning in the Mix editor. (https://support.spotify.com/us/article/mixed-playlists/)
- **Downloads are playlist-shaped, device-capped and leased month to month** — "You can't download individual songs", capacity is 10,000 tracks per device across at most 5 devices, and "You need to go online at least once every 30 days to keep your downloads." A paying subscriber who stays offline for a month loses the library they downloaded, and exceeding 5 devices silently wipes the least used one. (https://support.spotify.com/us/article/listen-offline/)
- **The web player offers no quality control whatsoever** — On the web player quality is fixed at AAC 128 kbit/s for Free and 256 kbit/s for Premium, and users can't adjust music quality on the web player. Lossless and 320 kbit/s require an installed app at a minimum version, so the browser is a permanently second-class client for both tiers. (https://support.spotify.com/us/article/audio-quality/)
- **AI features are English-first; most languages get nothing** — AI Playlist is English only across all 47 listed markets. Prompted Playlist is "currently only available in English". DJ reached just six languages by May 2026 (English, Spanish, French, German, Italian, Brazilian Portuguese). Spotify sells sign-ups in roughly 235 territories, so most listeners have no localized AI at all. (https://support.spotify.com/us/article/prompted-playlists/)
- **AI is metered, the limits are undisclosed, and overflow is monetized** — "There's a maximum amount that you can use in a set time period" with no published numbers, no stated reset window and no fixed cost per prompt. Overflow is sold as Pro credits with a 12-month expiry, no rollover and no refunds after 7 days, so a Premium subscription no longer bounds what a listener can actually do. (https://support.spotify.com/us/article/ai-usage-limits/)
- **Platform lottery: Studio is macOS-only, Exclusive mode is Windows-only** — Studio, the agent that builds personalized podcasts, is "only available on MacOS" and 18+. Exclusive mode, the bit-exact output path, exists only in the Windows desktop app. Prompted Playlist is mobile only. Which features a paying subscriber gets depends on which OS they happen to run. (https://support.spotify.com/us/article/studio/)
- **Audiobook hours expire, cannot be shared, and skip most plan members** — Unused hours "can no longer be used" after the month and are not refunded. On Duo and Family only the plan manager gets hours; Student gets none. The allowance differs by country (15 h US and UK, 12 h Brazil, none listed in India, Indonesia, Philippines, Nigeria), yet the bundle is priced into Premium everywhere. (https://support.spotify.com/us/article/audiobooks-premium-plans/)
- **Free is on-demand but still cannot control order or opt out of injection** — Free can start any song since 2025-09-15, yet the mobile play queue is marked "For: Premium", Smart Shuffle "is always active on mobile" with no documented off switch for Free, and Free can only join a Jam in person. Free listeners are forced to accept injected recommendations inside their own playlists. (https://support.spotify.com/us/article/shuffle-play/)
- **No cheap music-only tier for anyone new** — Basic, the music-only plan without audiobook hours, "is available for previously existing Premium subscribers" only, and Basic and Student users cannot buy add-ons. New subscribers in audiobook markets must pay the bundled price even if they never open an audiobook, and Platinum markets bundle AI features the same way. (https://support.spotify.com/us/article/spotify-basic/)

## Unverifiable

- WebSearch was unavailable for this turn (the run's shared search budget was already exhausted), so every finding comes from direct WebFetch of Spotify-owned pages on 2026-11-24. Spotify's app, web player and API were never touched.
- Spotify Free skip limits: no fetched Spotify page states any skip cap. The remembered "6 skips per hour" figure could not be sourced and may no longer exist after the September 2025 on-demand change.
- Whether Brazil Premium actually gets lossless. The BR plan page lists only "~320 kbps" and no BR-locale lossless support article resolved (both /br/ and /br-pt/ returned 404). Absence is inferred from marketing, not documented.
- The complete list of Premium Platinum markets. Confirmed present in IN, ID, ZA and AE and absent from US, GB, BR, PH and NG by direct storefront fetch; all other markets were unchecked.
- Exact AI usage quotas, reset windows and Pro credit prices. Spotify documents that limits and purchasable credits exist but publishes no numbers, so the real ceiling on AI DJ, Prompted Playlist and Studio use is unknown.
- The full DJ market list. Spotify says "over 75 markets" (2026-05-07) and names only eight new ones; no enumeration is published, and the support article says only that DJ "is available in a limited number of languages".
