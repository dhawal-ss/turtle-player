## Mechanics notes

LYRICS: authored in Musixmatch Pro (Japan: PetitLyrics); audio auto-fetches from a linked Premium account. The artist sets ONE timestamp PER LINE, anchored on the line's first word, main vocal prioritised when voices overlap. Rules: full repeats (no "x2"), #VERSE/#CHORUS tags, native script, lyrical audio only. Propagation takes days. Sync is therefore line-level, never word/syllable. Preview lines sit under album art or over the Canvas clip (mobile/tablet); 3-dot > Lyrics toggles them for "all tracks with available lyrics". Tap = full screen; share = share icon, tap lines to highlight, share sheet. Translation: translate icon on the lyrics card, target = device language, original restorable. Offline lyrics ride along with a track download, Premium only. CREDITS: label/distributor metadata, not editable by Spotify; Nov 2025 widened from topline roles to all contributors (producers, engineers, writers, featured). Fixes need redelivery, ~7 days. Songwriter names link out only where a publisher supplied CWR data creating a Songwriter Page + Written By playlist. AI credits (beta, mobile): voluntary per-element disclosure (lyrics/vocals/instrumentals/production) via participating distributors; absence proves nothing. SONGDNA (Premium, mobile): Now Playing card; Explore opens a traversable graph of credited contributors plus WhoSampled samples, interpolations and covers; tapping a creator expands their collaborators. Artist controls are desktop-only: all main artists show/hide third-party contributors and WhoSampled items; above 5,000 monthly active listeners they can also hide distributor-delivered contributors and add contributors, but only people already credited on Spotify. Main/featured artists and remixers cannot be hidden. Edits land within 48h and change SongDNA only, not View song credits. ABOUT THE SONG (Premium, app 9.1.6+): swipeable cards summarised from third-party sources, added automatically once a track passes a minimum stream count and enough reliable published third-party material exists; thumbs up/down feedback. CANVAS: 3-8s, 9:16, 720-1080px tall, MP4/JPG; loop styles continuous, hard cut, rebound. Listener switch: Settings > Content and display > Videos and Canvas, account-wide across mobile/desktop/web/TV; ads exempt. QUEUE: add, drag-reorder, swipe-remove, Clear. AUTOPLAY appends similar tracks when an album/playlist/selection ends (Settings > Playback).

## Spotify weaknesses

- **Line-level sync only; no word or syllable timing, no vocal reduction** — Spotify's own authoring guidance is one timestamp per line, anchored on the line's first word, and the 2021 launch describes lyrics that "scroll in real time". No Spotify page documents word- or syllable-level highlighting or a karaoke/vocal-reduction mode, unlike Apple Sing. Reviews treat this as Spotify's gap. (https://support.spotify.com/us/artists/article/managing-your-lyrics-on-spotify/)
- **No romanization for non-Latin scripts** — Lyrics must be transcribed in "the song's native script". There is no documented romanization view, so listeners who cannot read Hangul, kana, kanji, Cyrillic or Arabic script get a translation at best. The gap is filled by third-party browser extensions and Spicetify add-ons, and a long-running community idea thread. (https://support.spotify.com/us/artists/article/managing-your-lyrics-on-spotify/)
- **Listeners cannot add or correct lyrics; artists wait days** — "You can't add lyrics, but you can view them on Spotify." A wrong or missing lyric can only be fixed by the artist through Musixmatch Pro (or PetitLyrics in Japan), and "it may take a few days for the changes to reflect on Spotify." There is no in-app report-a-lyric path and no local override. (https://support.spotify.com/us/artists/article/managing-your-lyrics-on-spotify/)
- **Lyrics have been paywalled before and can be again** — In Sept 2023 Spotify tested restricting lyrics for free users in two markets with an "Enjoy lyrics on Spotify Premium" prompt; in May 2024 it quietly capped free users at roughly three songs of lyrics per month without announcing it, then raised the limit in July 2024 after complaints. Current availability is a policy, not a guarantee. (https://techcrunch.com/2024/05/02/spotify-is-quietly-moves-lyrics-behind-a-paywall/)
- **Offline lyrics are Premium-only** — Lyrics saved with a download are "rolling out around the world for Premium users". Free listeners with downloads cannot see lyrics offline at all, so the feature most useful without signal is tier-gated. (https://newsroom.spotify.com/2026-02-04/lyric-translations-offline-previews/)
- **Genius 'Behind the Lyrics' annotations were killed and never replaced in kind** — Behind the Lyrics launched with Genius in 2016 and Spotify confirmed in Nov 2021 that it was "sunsetting" it to make way for Lyrics. The replacement for song context arrived four years later as About the Song: Premium-only, market-limited, mobile-only, and summarised from third parties rather than annotated line by line. (https://techcrunch.com/2021/11/18/spotify-finally-rolls-out-real-time-lyrics-to-global-users/)
- **Storyline never shipped: no artist-authored song context** — Spotify began testing Storyline in May 2019 as artist-written cards on Now Playing. A Spotify community moderator later said it was "still in testing" and not a permanent feature, with no official info on whether it ever would be. Artists still have no documented way to write per-song context; only the 1,500-character profile bio. (https://community.spotify.com/t5/Other-Podcasts-Partners-etc/What-happened-to-the-Spotify-Storylines-feature/td-p/5594439)
- **Song context is fragmented across four cards with different gates** — Lyrics (Free+Premium, most platforms), Credits (mobile, all tiers), SongDNA (Premium, mobile) and About the Song (Premium, mobile, listed markets, app 9.1.6+) are separate Now Playing cards with different tiers, platforms and market coverage. There is no single unified "about this song" surface; what a listener sees depends on tier, market and app version. (https://support.spotify.com/us/artists/article/about-the-song/)
- **Credits are only as good as the distributor's metadata** — Spotify shows what the label or distributor delivers, "some distributors don't support credits yet", and a correction requires the distributor to resend metadata, appearing within about 7 days. Spotify cannot fix an error itself, and listeners have no way to flag a wrong credit. (https://support.spotify.com/us/artists/article/song-credits/)
- **Only songwriter credits are clickable, and only through publishers** — A credit becomes a link only where a publisher delivered CWR data through Spotify for Publishing to build a Songwriter Page; "not all songwriter credits are clickable just yet", and individual songwriters cannot request one. Producers and engineers get named in the expanded credits but have no documented destination page. (https://support.spotify.com/us/artists/article/songwriter-pages/)
- **SongDNA edits do not fix the real credits: two divergent truths** — "Changes made to SongDNA in Spotify for Artists appear only in the SongDNA experience", not in Now Playing Credits or View song credits. Contributor-adding is also gated at 5,000 monthly active listeners, new people cannot be added if never credited on Spotify before, and editing is desktop-only. Smaller artists get the least control. (https://support.spotify.com/us/artists/article/songdna/)
- **Canvas cannot be suppressed during ads, and specs cap expression** — Even with Canvas switched off, "Canvas visuals will still sometimes show with audio ads" on the free tier, so the opt-out does not hold where it matters most commercially. Canvas itself is capped at 3-8 seconds and 1080px tall, and Spotify advises against singing or talking footage, which rules out lyric-synced visuals. (https://support.spotify.com/us/article/video-settings/)

## Unverifiable

- Current status of any free-tier lyrics cap. No Spotify page states a limit, and the Feb 2026 post says previews and translations are for Free and Premium, but Spotify never announced the 2024 cap's removal.
- Whether tapping a lyric line seeks playback to that line. Claimed by third-party guides; not stated on any Spotify page.
- Whether Spotify offers any vocal-reduction or karaoke mode anywhere. A single low-quality source claims a Japan-only Joysound tie-in; no Spotify source supports it.
- Lyric font resizing in the full-screen view. Reported from a beta build by one outlet; not on Spotify's lyrics page.
- Storyline's current status. No official removal notice and no current help-centre article; the last Spotify word is a moderator saying it is still in testing.
- Canvas maximum file size, and whether Canvas is available in all markets. The guidelines page gives duration, ratio, height and format only.
- Whether About the Song cards are AI-generated. The page says only "summarized from third-party sources" and names no generation method or source vetting.
- Whether the expanded song credits reached desktop. Promised for desktop "over the coming months" in Nov 2025; no later Spotify statement found.
- Priority ordering between manually queued tracks, the playing context and Autoplay recommendations. Neither the queue nor the autoplay article states it.
- The order of cards in the mobile Now Playing view. Membership (lyrics, Canvas, Credits, SongDNA, About the Song, About the Artist) is documented across pages; the sequence is not.
