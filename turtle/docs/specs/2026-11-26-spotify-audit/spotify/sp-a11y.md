## Mechanics notes

TRANSCRIPTS: auto-generated every time an episode is published on Spotify (creators page, 9 Sep 2025). Chapters only "if you haven't already provided chapters"; guests/topics derived from the episode description. Creators edit chapter labels/timestamps, hide or rearrange tags, and opt out per episode or whole catalog; some features unlock by show status. The transcript sits in Now Playing, words highlight in time, and tapping a paragraph seeks there (2021 newsroom). Video podcasts: subtitles in the video window plus a separate "Read along" section, off via the 3-dot menu; for audio-only episodes the creator controls read-along and listeners reportedly cannot disable it. MOTION: one account-level "Videos and Canvas" off switch removes looping visuals, music videos and video podcasts on every device on the account - a global kill switch that removes content, not a per-surface control and not wired to the OS Reduce Motion flag. KEYBOARD: Tab/Shift+Tab traverse, Enter/Space activate, arrows move within lists, Esc closes menus. Navigation shortcuts are namespaced Alt+Shift+<key> (0 Library, 1 Playlists, ... H Home, J Now Playing, Q Queue), plus Cmd/Ctrl+K search and Ctrl/Cmd+/ help overlay. Bare Space, M and all four arrows carry playback and library actions - the collision noted in weaknesses. GOVERNANCE: 3-year plan, annual report, quarterly working-group check-ins, quarterly Advisory Board under the Accessibility Program Manager, who also receives every feedback-form submission (anonymous permitted). Built from a 100+ person disability survey plus two Fable focus groups (4 scoping, 5 reviewing the draft). Plan offered in print, large print, Braille and audio; requests answered within 15 days. Inferred: those seven areas, four formats and the 15-day clock match Accessible Canada Act plan structure, but no statute is named. TESTING: Fable Engage ran ~90 moderated tests in Jun 2025-Jun 2026 (~10/month) with screen reader, alternate-navigation and magnification users on iOS, Android and Web; each yields an Accessible Usability Scale score, so accessibility is tracked as a moving metric. Mobile audit findings are triaged blocking/critical/major/minor and reported as percent fixed. CI: accessibility PR review rules and a lint rule set across web-facing repos, a health dashboard, and an iOS pipeline approved to run every six hours (Android next). Encore enforces accessibility inside components where possible and documents it where stateless components cannot.

## Spotify weaknesses

- **No in-app text size, contrast, theme or reduced-motion control — everything is pushed to the OS** — The support page's in-app list is only mono audio, normalize volume, volume level, Videos/Canvas and transcripts. Text size, Smart Invert, high contrast text, colour inversion/correction, magnification and Reduce Motion are all listed as device settings, with availability 'depending on your device and OS version'. A listener on an older OS, a TV, a car head unit or a browser gets none of it. (https://support.spotify.com/us/article/accessibility/)
- **No light or high-contrast theme anywhere in the product** — Nothing on Spotify's accessibility or support pages documents a light theme or in-app contrast mode; the only route offered is OS inversion. Spotify's own community carries a long-running, still-open 'Light Mode option' idea thread where users with astigmatism and low vision describe halation, ghosting and eye strain on the dark UI, and fall back on Smart Invert or third-party Spicetify themes. (https://community.spotify.com/t5/Live-Ideas/All-Platforms-Light-Mode-option/idi-p/730341)
- **Desktop screen reader regressions ship in production releases** — A Premium user on Windows 11 reports that in client 1.2.86 search results and podcast episode lists lost the HTML headings marking sections like tracks and artists, a per-section ARIA region disappeared, and the top result exposes an ID instead of a readable accessible name. Reproduced with NVDA and JAWS. Spotify's reply: info 'passed internally to be reviewed', no fix or timeline. (https://community.spotify.com/t5/Desktop-Windows/Accessibility-Screen-reader-accessibility-issues-for-search/m-p/7396596)
- **Earlier, separate desktop regression in playlist view with NVDA and JAWS** — A distinct Windows 11 report says playlist view could no longer be navigated with NVDA or JAWS the way it previously could, appearing after an automatic update. Two independent navigation regressions on the same platform suggest desktop screen reader behaviour is not protected by any release gate. (https://community.spotify.com/t5/Desktop-Windows/Accessibility-Major-issue-can-t-navigate-playlist-view-with/td-p/6702404)
- **The Windows/Mac desktop app appears to be outside the documented testing programme** — The 2026 report's Fable Engage testing names iOS, Android and Web only, and the automated pipeline covers iOS with Android to follow — no desktop pipeline is mentioned. Inferred, since 'Web' may be read to cover the Electron-based desktop client; but the two desktop screen reader regressions above are consistent with desktop being the untested surface. (https://www.spotify.com/us/accessibility/2026-report)
- **No WCAG conformance level, conformance report or VPAT published** — Across the Accessibility Centre, the plan pages and the 2026 report, WCAG appears only as 'the most recent version' of the guidelines, with no version, no A/AA/AAA level and no per-criterion conformance claim. EN 301 549 and the European Accessibility Act are not named anywhere, and no VPAT or Accessibility Conformance Report surfaced in search. Nothing is auditable from outside. (https://www.spotify.com/us/accessibility/plan-introduction)
- **Remediation of known mobile defects stalls below 70% for major and minor issues** — Spotify's own 2026 report: 100% of blocking and 99% of critical mobile audit issues were remediated, but only about 60% of major and about 65% of minor issues. Major issues are by definition substantial barriers, and roughly two in five were still open at the end of the reporting year. (https://www.spotify.com/us/accessibility/2026-report)
- **Accessibility training reached about 5% of R&D** — The 2026 report states roughly 250 R&D employees, about 5%, completed accessibility training in the period, and that formal integration into the career framework is not started. The remaining 95% of the engineers and designers shipping the product have not been through it, which is the plausible upstream cause of the regressions above. (https://www.spotify.com/us/accessibility/2026-report)
- **Several first-year commitments were never started** — Per Spotify's own 2026 report: internal product audits not started, documentation writing standards 'not yet officially started', accommodations process review not started, Global Workplace Services accessibility training not started, key vendor accessibility assessments not started, and websites 'audited some, not all'. Marketing-asset audits are deferred to Q1 2027. (https://www.spotify.com/us/accessibility/2026-report)
- **Feedback channel is thin, single-threaded and has no response-time commitment** — The form received 56 submissions in the whole Jun 2025–Jun 2026 period (11 anonymous, 45 registered), all routed to one Program Manager, and the report lists 'additional feedback channels' as an open item with no date. The published process promises only to acknowledge receipt 'promptly' — no SLA, unlike the 15-day clock for plan-document requests. (https://www.spotify.com/us/accessibility/plan-feedback-process)
- **Transcript coverage is conditional and does not ingest other hosts' transcripts** — The listener-facing support page qualifies transcripts as available 'where available', varying by podcast and device, and the creator page ties some features to show status and lets creators opt out per episode or for a whole catalog. Spotify reportedly gave no timeframe for ingesting transcripts from other hosts' RSS feeds. Audiobooks and music videos have no documented text alternative. (https://creators.spotify.com/resources/grow/automated-transcripts-chapters)
- **Bare arrow keys are bound to playback, colliding with list navigation** — The shortcuts page prints Up Arrow as Go to Previous, Down Arrow as Go to Next, Left Arrow as Add to Library and Right Arrow as Add to Queue, with no modifier, while the accessibility page tells keyboard users to use arrow keys to move through lists. Inferred collision: a keyboard-only or screen reader user moving through a list risks a track change or a library write. (https://support.spotify.com/us/article/keyboard-shortcuts/)

## Unverifiable

- Whether Spotify publishes a VPAT or Accessibility Conformance Report anywhere. Searches surfaced only generic templates and other companies' reports; absence of evidence is not evidence of absence, and vendors often supply these on request only.
- Whether Spotify published an EU accessibility statement under the European Accessibility Act, which has applied since 28 June 2025. No such statement was found on spotify.com, and the Accessibility Centre never names the EAA or EN 301 549.
- Whether the dedicated Customer Service accessibility support team actually launched. The 2026 report says only that it was 'scheduled for May 2026'; no later confirmation was found.
- The legal basis of Spotify's Accessibility Plan. Its seven areas, alternate formats and 15-day response clock match Accessible Canada Act requirements, but no page names any law; the footer reads Spotify AB.
- Whether music videos on Spotify carry captions or subtitles. No Spotify help article on music-video captions was found; only video podcasts are documented.
- Whether lyrics (a text alternative for music) are gated to Premium, and in which markets. Not verified; the search budget ran out before this could be checked.
- Transcript language coverage. The 2021 beta was English-only for select Spotify Original and Exclusive shows; the 2025 creators page states no languages at all.
- Whether a light theme or in-app contrast mode shipped after 2025. A fetch of the light-mode idea thread returned status 'Live Idea' and no dates; a snippet suggesting a March 2023 'Not Right Now' moderator status could not be confirmed on the page.
- Any ADA lawsuit, regulatory complaint or enforcement action against Spotify over accessibility. Not searched; the web-search budget was exhausted before this query ran.
- Process limitation: the 200-call-per-turn WebSearch budget was exhausted mid-run, so the Accessible Canada Act basis, lyrics gating, lawsuits and Encore contrast/type-scale details were researched only via direct WebFetch of known URLs.
