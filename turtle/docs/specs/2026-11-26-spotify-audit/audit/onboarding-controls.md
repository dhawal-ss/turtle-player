## Weaknesses

### [BLOCKER] First run asks consent for crash reports Turtle then gives no way to switch off

- Evidence: OnboardingScreen.kt:91 / DesktopOnboarding.kt:114 add CRASH_REPORTS; App.kt:337-340 writes sentryEnabled and starts Sentry. Shell drops the switch: HIDE_PRIVACY = setOf("sentry") at ShellSettingsTurtle.kt:214-215
- Why it matters: Turtle's own comment says these reports go to Metrolist's servers. A listener who taps Yes on the first screen has consented to third-party diagnostics leaving their device forever: Shell's Privacy page has no toggle, and settings search only sees the reshaped registry (ShellSettingsTurtle.kt:180-184). Consent that cannot be withdrawn is worse than no prompt.
- Fix sketch: Either keep the "sentry" item in Shell's Privacy page (drop it from HIDE_PRIVACY in turtle/ui/.../ShellSettingsTurtle.kt:215 and relabel it to name the recipient), or force sentryEnabled=false as a Turtle default in turtle/bridge/.../TurtleDefaults.kt and remove the first-run page via hooks in androidApp/.../OnboardingScreen.kt:91 and desktopApp/.../DesktopOnboarding.kt:114.

### [BLOCKER] The one "just press play" path on a cold profile can end in silence with no message

- Evidence: ShellHome.kt:247-253: the first play opens the Infinity sheet. Start calls ShellInfinity.applyPicks (ShellInfinity.kt:220-225), which calls play(controller, mixTitle) with no onEmpty/onNothing; play returns silently on an empty mix (ShellInfinity.kt:159-166)
- Why it matters: shell_picks_card_body promises "Or just press play." With no picks and no genres, discoveryPick has no genre pools and falls to taste.forYou (InfinityMix.kt:692-718), empty on a profile with no history, so the sheet closes and nothing happens. Home's configured path does pass onEmpty/onNothing (ShellHome.kt:254). This is the first thing a new listener touches.
- Fix sketch: Pass the same onEmpty (quickPicks) and onNothing (notice) through ShellInfinity.applyPicks to play in turtle/ui/.../ShellInfinity.kt:220-225, and make the picks card's play button start something immediately rather than opening a second setup sheet (turtle/ui/.../ShellHome.kt:247-253).

### [BLOCKER] A fresh desktop install beside Metrolist inherits its consent and skips Turtle's first run

- Evidence: DesktopDataMigration.runAtStartup copies upstream's real homes (DesktopDataMigration.kt:100, :106-119) including config/metrolist (:134), which holds settings.properties. Main.kt:904-910 then reads desktopOnboardingInitialized/Complete and sentryEnabled from it
- Why it matters: Only updateCacheSource is dropped from the copied settings (DesktopDataMigration.kt:48). So a Metrolist user's sentryEnabled=true carries into Turtle, where the switch is hidden, and desktopOnboardingComplete=true means Turtle never shows its own setup. Consent given to one app silently becomes consent for another.
- Fix sketch: Drop the first-run and diagnostics keys (desktopOnboardingComplete, desktopOnboardingInitialized, desktopPendingOnboardingSentryConsent, sentryEnabled) alongside updateCacheSource in turtle/bridge/src/desktopMain/.../DesktopDataMigration.kt, so Turtle asks for itself.

### [MAJOR] The artist-picks offer is suppressed by any Turtle shelf, so it usually vanishes after one play

- Evidence: ShellHome.kt:474 shows the card only when lists.mixes.isEmpty(); HomeLayout.kt:183-185 counts every non-"More like" Turtle shelf as a mix. topArtistSeedsLocked has no minimum play count (TasteEngine.kt:936-948)
- Why it matters: sparse stays true to 50 finished plays, but a single play with positive affinity can seed an artist mix, and the state is re-read after every finished play (ArtistPicks.kt:134). The onboarding offer disappears almost immediately for the listeners it exists for, and is afterwards only findable four taps deep in Settings > Recommendations.
- Fix sketch: Base the card on picks.offer alone in turtle/ui/.../ShellHome.kt:472-483 and keep it above Made for you while sparse is true, or give it its own Home slot instead of sharing Made for you's. Optionally add a minimum finished-play threshold to topArtistSeedsLocked (TasteEngine.kt:936).

### [MAJOR] There is no Turtle-specific first-run step: setup is upstream's wizard

- Evidence: MainActivity.kt:310-346 and Main.kt:317-332 run upstream's OnboardingScreen/DesktopOnboarding; its pages (OnboardingScreen.kt:85-97) are migration, crash reports, notifications, language, playback, finish
- Why it matters: Turtle's whole differentiator is a private on-device recommender with real controls, and a new listener is told none of it. The first screen offers only Begin setup and a privacy link; artist picks surface later on Home, where a single play can hide them. Turtle's own roadmap names this gap (2026-11-23-discovery-release-roadmap.md:49).
- Fix sketch: Add one Turtle page to the shared OnboardingPage list (shared/.../ui/onboarding/OnboardingContent.kt), rendered by androidApp/.../OnboardingScreen.kt and desktopApp/.../DesktopOnboarding.kt: a short "nothing leaves this device" statement, the artist picker (reuse turtle/ui/.../ShellArtistPicks.kt), and a "Start listening" secondary action on WELCOME.

### [MAJOR] The picks sheet is a dead end offline or signed out

- Evidence: Suggestions come only from items already on Home (ShellArtistPicks.kt:135-142; ShellBrowse.kt:52-55 returns empty before Home draws); search returns empty offline (ArtistPicks.kt:68-76) and the sheet shows shell_picks_none_found (strings_picks.xml:17)
- Why it matters: On a slow or offline first launch, or when opened from Settings before Home has ever drawn, the single onboarding path is an empty search box reading "No artists found. Searching needs a connection." There is no bundled or genre-diverse starter set to fall back to.
- Fix sketch: Give ShellArtistPicks.suggestionsOf a fallback when onScreen is empty: the listener's library and liked artists via LibrarySync, then the Infinity genre seeds, in turtle/ui/.../ShellArtistPicks.kt:135-142 and turtle/bridge/.../ArtistPicks.kt. Also show an offline state with a retry rather than a bare "none found".

### [MAJOR] Saving picks from Settings shows no progress and never reports failure

- Evidence: ShellArtistPicks.saving is read only by the Home card (ShellArtistPicks.kt:178-181); the Settings row shows only the saved names (ShellSettingsTurtle.kt:390-391). Errors are logged and swallowed (ArtistPicks.kt:222-227, TurtleModule.kt:343-354)
- Why it matters: save() makes about two YouTube calls per artist plus a graph rebuild bounded at 10 s (ArtistPicks.kt:169-185). Edited from Settings, the row keeps showing the old picks the whole time, with no spinner and no error if every artist failed offline. The listener cannot tell whether their choice took effect.
- Fix sketch: Read ShellArtistPicks.saving in the Settings row description and surface a failure count from TurtleArtistPicks.save in turtle/ui/.../ShellSettingsTurtle.kt:436-447 and turtle/bridge/.../ArtistPicks.kt:169-185 (return a small result instead of Unit).

### [MAJOR] Artist demotion from "Less like this" is invisible and cannot be reset

- Evidence: UserControls.apply adds a mark to lessLikeThisArtists on every track-level Less like this (UserControls.kt:81-85); hidden() lists only tracks, muted artists and shelves (UserControls.kt:105-108), so no Hidden row exists for marks
- Why it matters: One mark already removes an artist from artist-mix seeds (TasteEngine.kt:941) and two marks drop any Home row containing them (HomeRows.kt:214). A listener who skipped three songs by an artist they otherwise like has silently removed them from Made for you, with nothing in the Hidden page to show it and no way back short of undoing each song.
- Fix sketch: Add a Hidden.ArtistMarks case to turtle/engines/taste/.../UserControls.kt:93-128 (hidden() and undo()), publish it as a fourth group in TurtleRuntime.publishHiddenNow (TurtleRuntime.kt:601-631) and render it with a "Show again" that clears the marks in turtle/ui/.../ShellHidden.kt:60-78.

### [MAJOR] Two independent hide systems appear on the same long-press, with separate undo pages

- Evidence: Turtle's "Not interested" row is injected into upstream's menu header (ShellMenus.kt:159, ShellHomeFeedback.kt:86-133); upstream's "Hide from home" sits in the same menu (MediaMenuModel.kt:542). Undo lives at ShellHidden.kt:48 vs PrivacySettingsRegistration.kt:186-193
- Why it matters: Long-pressing an artist on Home offers two similar-sounding negatives with different effects (taste mute vs home filter) and different recovery routes. Blocked artists is a third list (PrivacySettingsRegistration.kt:178-184). A listener cannot predict which to use or where to find what they hid.
- Fix sketch: Decide one model: either filter upstream's hide and block actions out of Shell-rendered menus (in turtle/ui/.../ShellMenus.kt around the action slots), or have ShellHidden.kt list upstream's blocked and hidden entries alongside Turtle's, using the data turtle/bridge/.../UpstreamBlocks.kt already reads.

### [MAJOR] Dismissing a mix is keyed by its current top artist, so the dismissal drifts

- Evidence: Reason.DailyMix.key = "mix:${topArtists.firstOrNull() ?: clusterId}" (turtle/engines/taste/.../Candidates.kt:228-229); cluster mixes pass live cluster top artists (TasteEngine.kt:1012) and the key is stored in suppressedReasons (TurtleRuntime.kt:511-515)
- Why it matters: "Not interested" on a Daily Mix is remembered by whichever artist led the cluster at that moment. When clusters are recomputed and the lead changes, the dismissal stops matching and the mix returns, or starts hiding a different mix whose lead happens to match. ClusterAnchor and muteCluster exist for exactly this (UserControls.kt:33-43) and are unused.
- Fix sketch: Route mix dismissal through taste.muteCluster / ClusterAnchor for cluster mixes in turtle/bridge/.../TurtleRuntime.kt:511-515, keeping the reason key only for non-cluster shelves, and show the anchor's current title on the Hidden page.

### [MAJOR] Infinity genre and mood picks search fixed English phrases

- Evidence: turtle/bridge/.../InfinityMix.kt:73 (InfinityGenre(id, query)) and :78-96: "pop hits", "hip hop hits", "lofi hip hop beats", "classical music essentials", hardcoded
- Why it matters: This is the first personalization most new listeners touch, since the first play opens it. A Hindi, Japanese or Portuguese listener picking "pop" gets an English query, biasing their whole first session toward Western catalogue. It also breaks the repository's own rule against hardcoded English defaults (CLAUDE.md, Strings and locale).
- Fix sketch: Derive the query from the listener's content language, or stop using text search for genres: point each pick at the YouTube Music mood/genre browse ids that turtle/bridge/.../TurtleExplore.kt already loads, with the English phrase only as a last resort. File: turtle/bridge/.../InfinityMix.kt:73-110.

### [MAJOR] "Not interested" exists only on Home; everywhere else offers a differently named control

- Evidence: The latch is armed only by ShellHomeFeedback.openMenu (ShellHomeFeedback.kt:69-79) and claimed by a 3 s exact-title match (NotInterested.kt:119-124). Player, queue, Infinity and sheets call ShellActions.lessLikeThis instead (ShellPlayer.kt:570, ShellInfinity.kt:461, ShellPlayerSheets.kt:446)
- Why it matters: The same underlying action has two names depending on where you are, and only the Home version can target an artist. A listener who hears an artist they dislike in a radio queue has no way to mute them without finding that artist on Home. The title-match latch is also fragile: any menu opening with a matching title within 3 s claims the target.
- Fix sketch: Expose one labelled control everywhere: offer "Not interested in this song" and "Not interested in this artist" from the player and queue menus, routed through TurtleBridge.notInterested, in turtle/ui/.../ShellPlayerSheets.kt and ShellQueue.kt, and replace the title latch with the item identity passed through the menu slot.

### [MAJOR] No way to remove a single play from what Turtle learned

- Evidence: turtle/engines/events/.../PlayEventStore.kt:36-42 exposes only replaceSource/deleteSource per import source; MetrolistHistoryImport.kt:27-44 has already copied upstream's plays into Turtle's log
- Why it matters: Deleting an embarrassing play from upstream's History screen does not remove it from Turtle's taste log, because the copy was taken at first launch. The only remedies are Reset taste (all of it) or nothing. For a product whose pitch is listener control over an on-device profile, there is no delete.
- Fix sketch: Add deleteEvents(ids) and deleteRange(fromMs, toMs) to turtle/engines/events/.../PlayEventStore.kt and turtle/bridge/.../SqlitePlayEventStore.kt, reuse TurtleRuntime.historyReadingChanged for the rebuild, and surface them from a Turtle listening-history page linked from Settings > Recommendations.

### [MAJOR] No per-song or per-session taste exclusion, only an all-or-nothing pause

- Evidence: UserControls.kt:46-60 has no excluded-session or excluded-track field; the only control is Settings.pauseListenHistory (PrivacySettingsRegistration.kt:64-66, TurtleModule.kt:83). Designed but unbuilt: 2026-11-22-paper-gap-review.md:33, :158
- Why it matters: Handing the phone to a child, or playing one genre for a party, pollutes the profile unless the listener remembers to pause history beforehand and resume after. Pausing also stops Jump back in and all learning, so the honest option costs them the features they came for.
- Fix sketch: Implement the B4 primitive: UserControls.excludedSessions / excludedFromTaste with TasteState lines, a profile rebuild in TasteEngine, TurtleRuntime.excludeSession/excludeTrack, a player-sheet row and an "Excluded from taste" group on the Hidden page (turtle/engines/taste/.../UserControls.kt, TasteEngine.kt, turtle/bridge/.../TurtleRuntime.kt, turtle/ui/.../ShellPlayerSheets.kt, ShellHidden.kt).

### [MAJOR] A complete history-import engine has no listener-facing entry point

- Evidence: Importers, atomic commit and previews exist (SpotifyHistoryImporter.kt:78, ListenBrainzHistoryImporter.kt:67, HistoryImport.kt:103-148, TurtleRuntime.kt:688-697, bound at TurtleModule.kt:112-113) but no file in turtle/ui references stage or commitHistoryImport
- Why it matters: The strongest answer to a cold start is already built and unreachable: a listener arriving from another service cannot bring their history, so they get the picks card instead, which a single play then hides. The engine also counts duplicates, non-music and private-session rows for a preview nobody can see.
- Fix sketch: Add the platform file readers (SAF OpenDocument plus ZipInputStream in turtle/bridge/src/androidMain, a file dialog in turtle/bridge/src/desktopMain), both counting uncompressed chars against ImportLimits.maxFileChars, then a preview-and-confirm screen in turtle/ui linked from Settings > Recommendations and from the picks sheet.

- Why it matters: A listener who nudges the dial expecting it to touch only one mix is also changing autoplay, radio and Made for you. On desktop the card gives no numbers at all, so the same control is legible on the phone and opaque on the computer.
- Fix sketch: Reword shell_set_recs_dial_body in turtle/ui/src/commonMain/composeResources/values/strings_settings.xml:36 to name radio, autoplay and Made for you, and drop captions=false at turtle/ui/.../ShellDesktop.kt:699 or give the desktop panel a compact readout.

### [MINOR] Picks chips and genre chips expose no selected state to screen readers

The same omission is already known for the theme palette cards.

### [MINOR] The picks sheet loses work silently and has three quiet dead ends

- Evidence: A 6th pick is a silent no-op (ShellArtistPicks.kt:243-247); Done with nothing chosen calls save(emptyList()) (:305), clearing picks without dismissing so the card returns (ArtistPicks.kt:52); an outside tap dismisses the dialog, discarding chosen (:249)
- Why it matters: Tapping a 6th artist does nothing with no explanation. Pressing Done with none chosen returns the listener to the same card with no reason given. An accidental tap outside throws away five deliberate choices with no confirmation. All three are first-session interactions, where confidence matters most.
- Fix sketch: In turtle/ui/.../ShellArtistPicks.kt: disable or explain the limit when chosen.size == max, make Done inert or treat it as Skip when chosen is empty, and confirm before dismissing an edited sheet.

### [MINOR] Reachable privacy and Home controls that do not govern Turtle's learning or shelves

- Evidence: historyDuration (PrivacySettingsRegistration.kt:88-97) and hiddenHomeItems / homeSectionVisibility (HomeShelfBuilder.kt:477) have no reader anywhere under turtle/; Turtle qualifies plays by its own thresholds (PlayEvent.kt:99-111)
- Why it matters: A listener who sets "count a play after 30 seconds" or hides a Home section reasonably assumes it governs what Turtle learns and shows. - Fix sketch: Either read Settings.historyDuration in turtle/bridge/.../PlayerObserver.kt when deciding what to record and apply hiddenHomeItems / homeSectionVisibility in HomeLayout.of (turtle/bridge/.../HomeLayout.kt:178-196), or drop those items from Shell's registry and state in Settings > Recommendations what actually governs Turtle's learning.
