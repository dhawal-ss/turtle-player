## Weaknesses

### [BLOCKER] One malformed byte in the saved taste state discards every listener control

- Evidence: TasteState.kt:48-75 is one runCatching returning null. lessArtists does `val (a, n) = p.split(PAIR)` then `n.toInt()` (TasteState.kt:61-64); anchors does `a.split(',').map { it.toFloat() }` (TasteState.kt:56). Caller only logs (TurtleRuntime.kt:734).
- Why it matters: A single bad field loses every hidden song, muted artist, dismissed mix, onboarding pick and the exploration posterior at once. The listener's explicit "never show me this" is user data, and losing it silently is the worst failure here. Kept shelves are already guarded individually (TasteState.kt:73); the controls are not.
- Fix sketch: In turtle/engines/taste/.../TasteState.kt wrap each field in its own runCatching, as KeptShelvesCodec already is, and keep whatever parses. Return a skipped-field count and log it from TurtleRuntime.kt:734 instead of dropping the line. Keep TurtleRuntime's separate hidden-song records as the recovery path.

### [BLOCKER] The engine's own Up Next, autoplay and seeded-radio pipeline has no caller

- Evidence: TasteEngine.kt:286-316, 409-416, 644-658, 856-880. Grep across turtle/, androidApp/, desktopApp/, shared/ finds no production caller. Production uses only rankExternal (TurtleRuntime.kt:302, SmartShuffleBridge.kt:70, InfinityMix.kt:824), forYou and homeShelves.
- Why it matters: The seven generators, the slot plan, artist spacing, album caps and the frozen two-song head never build the queue a listener hears. Turtle can only reorder what YouTube's page already contained, so it can never surface a song that page omitted. That is exactly where a catalogue-wide autoplay wins.
- Fix sketch: Either wire it in turtle/bridge/.../TurtleRuntime.kt (on an exhausted or absent upstream page call upNext/startAutoplay and append engine picks, tagged PlayPlace.ENGINE), or delete the dead surface and its config (TasteEngine.kt:286-316, 409-416, 856-880; QueueRules.frozenCount, Queue.kt:15-16) so the code stops describing a pipeline that never runs.

### [MAJOR] HEARD_BEFORE ignores saved songs and twin uploads, so favourites are served as discoveries

- Evidence: Features.kt:148 uses only `ts.plays > 0`, while hasHeard also counts `saved` and twin identities (TasteProfile.kt:218-221). SlotKind derives from HEARD_BEFORE (Ranker.kt:107) and the exploration posterior is updated from that slot (TasteEngine.kt:220-221).
- Why it matters: A song liked on YouTube Music but never played here has affinity 2.0 (TasteProfile.kt:362), so it ranks high, is labelled DISCOVERY, fills a discovery slot, and its easy finish is booked as a discovery success. The learned share then drifts up on evidence containing no discovery, and "all new" serves the listener their own library.
- Fix sketch: In turtle/engines/taste/.../Features.kt:148-149 set HEARD_BEFORE from profile.hasHeard(candidate.track) and ARTIST_HEARD_BEFORE from hasHeardArtist, which is already the rule weeklyDiscoveryLocked uses (TasteEngine.kt:1072). If saved-but-unplayed songs should still rank high, add a separate SAVED feature rather than reusing HEARD_BEFORE.

### [MAJOR] Infinity tags every genre and more-like pick DISCOVERY, even songs played twice

- Evidence: InfinityMix.kt:461-466 hardcodes SlotKind.DISCOVERY for Genre and MoreLike picks. The freshness gate allows two prior plays: InfinityMix.kt:582-584, `if (stats != null) stats.plays < 3`.
- Why it matters: Infinity is the continuous mix listeners leave running, so it produces most of the exploration evidence. Finishing a song already played twice is recorded as a discovery success, pushing the Beta posterior and the discovery share up. The learned share stops measuring discovery at all.
- Fix sketch: In turtle/bridge/.../InfinityMix.kt:461-466 derive the slot from the profile (hasHeard gives FAMILIAR) instead of hardcoding DISCOVERY, and require plays == 0 at InfinityMix.kt:582-584 for anything tagged DISCOVERY, keeping plays < 3 only as pool eligibility.

### [MAJOR] Two unrelated discovery-share mechanisms; the learned one never reaches the mix

- Evidence: Engine: Thompson draw clamped by the dial (Exploration.kt:114-116; TasteEngine.kt:364, 428, 876). Infinity: linear `0.85 - 0.75 * position` with no posterior (InfinityMix.kt:70, used at InfinityMix.kt:375-376, 385-392). The caption shows Infinity's number (ShellPlayer.kt:635, 664-676).
- Why it matters: One dial produces two behaviours, and the "X% familiar / Y% new" caption is true only for Infinity. Everything the engine learned about this listener's appetite is discarded on the surface they use most, which turns the honest-numbers promise into a half-truth.
- Fix sketch: In turtle/bridge/.../InfinityMix.kt:375-376 take the share from the engine (expose the sampled share, or reuse forYou's slot plan) instead of infinityFamiliarShare. If both must stay, say in ShellPlayer.kt:664-676 that the number is the mix's target while ranked surfaces adapt. Ship one mechanism, not two.

### [MAJOR] POPULARITY and FRESHNESS are never populated, so two documented features are inert

- Evidence: SongItemMapping.kt:11-23 sets neither popularity nor releasedAtMs, both declared at Track.kt:81-86. Only YouTubeFreshReleases.kt:130 sets releasedAtMs, and to firstSeenMs. Features.kt:151-158, 176; weights Ranker.kt:61, 73.
- Why it matters: POPULARITY is a constant 0.5 for every candidate, so Ranker.kt:19-21's "personal over popular" describes nothing. FRESHNESS is 0 and FRESHNESS_DAYS -1 outside Fresh for You, so new releases get no lift anywhere else. InMemoryTrackCatalog's popularity sort (Seams.kt:81) degenerates to a key sort.
- Fix sketch: In turtle/bridge/.../SongItemMapping.kt:14-22 carry a popularity proxy (view or play count, or upstream rank position) and a release date where InnerTubeX supplies one. If neither is obtainable, drop POPULARITY's weight at Ranker.kt:73 and correct its KDoc rather than scoring a constant.

### [MAJOR] Part confidence is a no-op, and every song by an artist collapses to one vector

- Evidence: TrackRepresentation.kt:75-85 normalizes a single part, scales by w*confidence, then renormalizes, so 0.6 cancels. Production has one part (TasteEngine.kt:81; TurtleModule.kt:122-124). An unremembered song falls back to the plain artist vector (TrackRepresentation.kt:33-36).
- Why it matters: SESSION_SIM (1.6), CLUSTER_SIM (1.4) and especially SEED_SIM (3.5) cannot tell two songs by one artist apart, so ordering inside an artist rests only on history and fatigue. The artist-fallback discount meant to express lower confidence has no effect, so uncertain vectors are treated as certain.
- Fix sketch: In turtle/engines/taste/.../TrackRepresentation.kt:75-85 apply confidence by shrinking vector length and stop renormalizing a lone part, or return confidence from vectorFor and multiply the cosine features by it in Features.kt:133-141. Raise TRACK_SELF (CoListening.kt:259) so remembered songs separate within an artist.

### [MAJOR] 64 dimensions with 8-nonzero random indexing is undersized for 2000 artists

- Evidence: EMBEDDING_DIM 64 (Vectors.kt:6); SIGNATURE_NONZEROS 8 and `(h ushr 1) % dim` (CoListening.kt:242-257); maxArtists 2000 (CoListening.kt:23). Two unrelated 8-nonzero signatures collide on about one of 64 bins, so cosine noise is of order 0.1.
- Why it matters: That noise enters CLUSTER_SIM (1.4), SESSION_SIM (1.6) and SEED_SIM (3.5), up to a few tenths of a logit of pure randomness per candidate, the same order as a real affinity difference. Clusters, radio neighbours and the also-play set all inherit it, and the listener feels a mix that wanders for no reason.
- Fix sketch: Raise EMBEDDING_DIM (Vectors.kt:6) to 128-256 with SIGNATURE_NONZEROS to match (CoListening.kt:257), and gate the change on cluster stability measured through the existing OfflineReplayTest. The pack format already carries dim per file (EmbeddingPack.kt:113-115), so nothing on the wire is frozen.

### [MAJOR] The whole candidate universe is about twelve thousand songs

- Evidence: CoListening.kt:23-25: maxArtists 2000, maxNeighbours 40, maxTracksPerArtist 6. Track vectors exist only for remembered songs (CoListening.kt:203-211), so nearestTracks scans ~12k rows and nearestArtists ~2000. CatalogWithObserved adds only the library (CoListening.kt:269-279).
- Why it matters: Every vector-based surface (Weekly Discovery, cluster mixes, More like X, radio reranking) can propose only from those songs plus whatever YouTube's page contained. A listener two months in keeps meeting the same neighbour artists, which is the clearest gap against a catalogue-wide recommender.
- Fix sketch: Raise maxTracksPerArtist and maxArtists in turtle/engines/taste/.../CoListening.kt:22-26 against measured memory, and ship the planned artist-level pack so the artist space covers the catalogue (EmbeddingPack.kt exists, the downloader does not). Add the ANN path behind EmbeddingStore.kt:41-48 before raising the ceiling far.

### [MAJOR] Graph eviction drops niche favourites before popular strangers

- Evidence: CoListening.kt:119-124 evicts by lowest total edge weight once above maxArtists. Radio-page edges are worth up to 1.0 (CoListening.kt:50, 134-135); a back-to-back finish is worth 0.5 (CoListening.kt:63, 136). The profile is never consulted.
- Why it matters: An artist the listener plays but who appears in few radio pages has a small total and is evicted, while an artist seen in forty pages and never played survives. Their vector disappears, their songs lose cluster and session support, and taste quietly erodes toward whatever YouTube shows most.
- Fix sketch: Give trim an optional protected set in turtle/engines/taste/.../CoListening.kt:109-131 (the profile's positive-affinity artists plus onboarding picks, passed in from CoListeningSync or TasteEngine) and exclude it from both the neighbour and the artist eviction passes.

### [MAJOR] Profile rebuilds hold the mutex that ranking needs within 1500 ms

- Evidence: Per play: TasteEngine.kt:222, 802-805 to TasteProfile.kt:456-509 rebuilds all TrackStats, all ArtistStats, up to 1000 vectorFor calls and the whole heardSongs set. Every 15 min: CoListeningSync.kt:73-77 to TasteEngine.kt:201-204, 787-800 re-reads 730 days and reruns k-means.
- Why it matters: rankExternal waits on the same mutex (TasteEngine.kt:339) against a 1500 ms budget (TurtleRuntime.kt:295, 785). During a rebuild the radio page times out and returns in YouTube's order as Served.TIMEOUT (TurtleRuntime.kt:313-319), so personalization drops out exactly while the graph is learning fastest.
- Fix sketch: In turtle/engines/taste/.../TasteEngine.kt:787-805 build the new profile outside the mutex and swap it in under the lock. Make updateProfileLocked incremental so a play touches only its own track and artist instead of rebuilding every TrackStats, and maintain heardSongs incrementally in TasteProfileBuilder.

### [MAJOR] SEED_SIM at 3.5 collapses radio toward the seed artist and its neighbours

- Evidence: Ranker.kt:78 (3.5) against SESSION_SIM 1.6 and LONG_TERM_SIM 1.0. The seed vector is the seed track's (TasteEngine.kt:352), i.e. its artist vector plus 0.25 of a track signature (CoListening.kt:203-211), so SEED_SIM is effectively same-or-neighbour artist. maxPerArtist is unlimited (Queue.kt:21-22).
- Why it matters: A cosine swing of one moves the logit by 3.5, outweighing a clear affinity gap, a bad artist skip rate and a less-like-this mark together. A station started from one song is re-sorted into a tight ring around that artist, so Turtle's radio can feel narrower than the YouTube page it started from.
- Fix sketch: Lower SEED_SIM in turtle/engines/taste/.../Ranker.kt:78 toward SESSION_SIM's range and express seed closeness as a retrieval-side floor instead, or cap maxPerArtist for ranked external pages at TasteEngine.kt:367. Validate with OfflineReplayTest's novelty and coverage metrics before settling the number.

### [MAJOR] One "will it finish" objective serves discovery, rediscovery and fresh shelves alike

- Evidence: Ranker.kt:13-14 defines the target as P(completed, not skipped). Every shelf calls the same rankLocked: TasteEngine.kt:1070-1073 (weekly discovery), 1121-1127 (fresh), 1129-1134 (rediscover), 1113-1117 (on repeat).
- Why it matters: A never-heard song has zero affinity, zero heard-before and no fatigue, so its score is almost entirely cluster and session similarity. The model best at predicting a finish is the model that predicts familiarity, so the discovery shelf is ranked by the one objective guaranteed to prefer safe picks.
- Fix sketch: HandTunedRanker already takes its weights by constructor, so add a discovery weight set in turtle/engines/taste/.../Ranker.kt and pass it per shelf from TasteEngine.kt:1070-1134. Measure with ReplayEvaluator's existing NEW_ONLY target (eval/ReplayEvaluator.kt:31-36) rather than the ALL_FINISHED one.

### [MAJOR] A single song mark quietly penalises a whole artist, at three thresholds, invisibly

- Evidence: UserControls.kt:81-85 bumps lessLikeThisArtists on every track mark. Effects: -0.7 per mark (Ranker.kt:76); Smart Shuffle blocks at one mark (SmartShuffle.kt:92); Home rows disliked at two (HomeRows.kt:58, 214); mix seed dropped at one (TasteEngine.kt:941). Not in hidden() (UserControls.kt:105).
- Why it matters: Marking one song you dislike by an artist you love removes that artist from Smart Shuffle recommendations outright, and the listener cannot see or undo that except by finding the original song in the Hidden list. Three thresholds for one signal make the behaviour unpredictable, which undermines the promise that the controls work.
- Fix sketch: Settle on one constant. Make SmartShuffle.kt:92 compare against HomeRowConfig.dislikedArtistMarks instead of testing map membership, and do the same at TasteEngine.kt:941. Surface the derived artist penalty from UserControls.hidden() (UserControls.kt:105-108) so the Hidden page can show and undo it.

### [MAJOR] Per-item "why this song" never reaches a listener although the data is there

- Evidence: Candidates.kt:166-256 defines sixteen typed reasons on every Candidate and QueueItem. Rendering covers shelf titles plus three BecauseYou cases (TurtleHomeShelves.kt:245-258); the only per-item label is Infinity's four categories (ShellInfinity.kt:265-278).

- Fix sketch: In turtle/bridge/.../TurtleRuntime.kt keep a songId-to-Reason map beside the existing surface tags for items Turtle served and expose TurtleBridge.why(songId). Render it in turtle/ui/.../ShellPlayerSheets.kt and the Home item menu with new keys in shared/src/commonMain/composeResources/values/strings.xml, plus a "Not for this reason" action calling lessLikeThis.

### [MAJOR] Mood and cluster mute/boost, the strongest steering controls, have no Shell entry point

## Strengths

- **Every weight, threshold and decay is readable code on the listener's own device** — Ranker.kt:49-96, TasteProfile.kt:19-47, Exploration.kt:63-84, Queue.kt:8-23, HomeRows.kt:47-72. The profile is built from the local event store (TasteEngine.kt:787-800); vectors and history stay on device (CoListeningSync.kt:98-119). | beats Spotify: The taste model lives only on the phone and the scoring function is auditable line by line. A hosted recommender keeps both the profile and the weights server-side, where the person being modelled cannot inspect either.
- **The familiar/new balance is learned per listener and bounded by the listener** — Thompson posterior over discovery outcomes (Exploration.kt:79-116) clamped by an explicit five-stop dial (UserControls.kt:10-29; ShellPlayer.kt:620-676), shown with its resulting percentages (ShellPlayer.kt:664-676). | beats Spotify: The listener sets the bounds and sees the numbers; the system adapts only inside them. Published research on familiarity/discovery balancing treats it as a system-side objective, not a user control.
- **Negative controls are hard exclusions applied on every path, ranked or not** — eligibleExternal is pure and lock-free so the unranked fallback applies the same rule (ExternalEligibility.kt:10-33); also Generators.kt:349-352, TasteEngine.kt:357, 887, 575-578 and SmartShuffle.kt:90-94. | beats Spotify: "Not interested" removes the song and its other uploads everywhere, at once and durably, instead of acting as a soft down-weight that decays back.
- **One song is one song, whichever upload it came from** — SongIdentity.kt:15-29 canonicalises artist plus title with version markers; used at Queue.kt:98-103, 130, TasteProfile.kt:209-221, TurtleRuntime.kt:299-302 and HomeRows.kt:288-295. | beats Spotify: On YouTube's catalogue the same recording exists as an official video and an audio-only upload. Turtle offers neither twice and counts a heard twin as heard, which provider-id dedupe cannot do.
- **Shelves are dropped rather than padded, and the known share is stated honestly** — minFallbackShelfSize 5 (TasteEngine.kt:52, 993, 1089); empty builds are retried, never kept (TasteEngine.kt:583-585); MIN_ITEMS 4 (TurtleHomeShelves.kt:262); the real known share is computed and shown (TurtleHomeShelves.kt:159; ShellMixDetails.kt:182-205). | beats Spotify: A mix that cannot be built does not appear, and a mix that is mostly unfamiliar says so rather than carrying a made-for-you label it has not earned.
- **No promoted slots: provider picks carry a handicap so personal picks win ties** — sourceBias UPSTREAM_RADIO -0.2, REDISCOVERY +0.1 (Ranker.kt:92-96). Every generator draws from history, the library or YouTube's radio (Generators.kt:65-297). Candidate has no sponsorship or placement field (Candidates.kt:262-270). | beats Spotify: There is no ad inventory and no placement field, so nothing can buy a position, and at equal predicted fit the listener's own history beats the provider's suggestion.
- **Failures degrade gracefully and are labelled rather than hidden** — Per-generator timeout and exception isolation (Generators.kt:336-344); RadioCache bounds network calls to five seeds (HistoryFallback.kt:20-50); fallbacks are tagged Served.TIMEOUT, FAILED or PROVIDER (TurtleRuntime.kt:313-319, 390-425; Candidates.kt:61-81). | beats Spotify: When personalization could not run the page still plays and the reason is written on the play, so later measurement never credits provider order to the ranker.
- **Full per-play attribution, on device, for honest measurement** — SurfaceTag format place[+served][@position]/source/slot[/row] (Candidates.kt:99-153), written at TurtleRuntime.kt:304, 416, SmartShuffleBridge.kt:79, TurtleHomeShelves.kt:213, InfinityMix.kt:409 and read back at TasteProfile.kt:432-434. | beats Spotify: The instrumentation that normally exists only in a provider's warehouse sits on the listener's device and feeds nothing but their own model.
- **An offline evaluation harness and listener-outcome gates live in the repository** — eval/ReplayEvaluator.kt:38-131 (capped and standard recall, NDCG, novelty as self-information, coverage); eval/ListenerOutcomes.kt (first-five skips with an unknown-rate caveat at :43-44, 7-day discovery retention); eval/OriginReport.kt with a local probe. | beats Spotify: The quality bar and its caveats are published and runnable rather than internal to a vendor, so a claim about discovery quality can be checked by anyone with the source.
- **Reproducible clustering: the same history always gives the same mixes** — Fixed seed 0x7A57E with key-sorted input (TasteProfile.kt:242-243, 525-530); clusters re-ranked deterministically by weight then first artist (TasteProfile.kt:555-566); mute and boost anchored on centroids, not ids (UserControls.kt:32-43). | beats Spotify: Daily Mixes keep their identity across rebuilds, so a control the listener applied to one side of their taste still means the same thing tomorrow.
- **Exploration refuses to flatter itself** — Exploration.kt:88-99: an imported play, a replay or a step back to a track is NEUTRAL, and the KDoc states the cost (a real success on a seek-back is dropped too). Only the pick's own locally started play can count. | beats Spotify: Choosing to hear a song again is not scored as the recommender succeeding, so the discovery share cannot ratchet up on evidence the listener produced by hand.
- **Spacing, fatigue and album caps are never relaxed to fill a queue** — Queue.kt:69-73 states the rule, Queue.kt:144-148 implements it: when nothing fits the loop breaks and the queue is shorter. artistSpacing 3, maxPerAlbumPerHour 2, minRepeatGapMs 3 h (Queue.kt:8-23), projected over real durations (Queue.kt:110-121). | beats Spotify: A mix runs short rather than repeating an artist or an album, which targets the complaint listeners make most about long autoplay sessions.
