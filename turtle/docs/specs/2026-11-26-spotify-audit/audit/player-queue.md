## Weaknesses

### [MAJOR] Starting a radio keeps the previous queue's Turtle identity, so the player lies about what is playing

- Evidence: ShellQueue.kt:34 active() only drops the anchor when its song leaves the queue; ShellQueue.kt:163-168 startRadio never clears it; ShellPlayer.kt:520 infinityOn compares the anchor's title
- Why it matters: Start radio from the song that began the queue and the radio queue still contains that song, so the anchor stays alive: the player keeps Infinity thumbs, the tune-on-tap source name and 'why this song' labels for a radio the Infinity engine did not build, and feedback is attributed to the wrong surface.
- Fix sketch: Add a clear() to ShellQueue that nulls anchor and source and call it from ShellActions.startRadio (turtle/ui/.../screens/ShellQueue.kt:163) before controller.startRadio. Better: key the anchor on the queue occurrence that TurtleBridge.beginOccurrence already returns, not on a song id.

### [MAJOR] Infinity controls disappear after an app restart while the header still claims the Infinity Mix

- Evidence: ShellQueue.kt:24 anchor is a memory-only mutableStateOf; androidApp/.../MetrolistApp.kt:2095 and AndroidPlaybackPolicies.kt:634 restore pending.queueTitle; ShellPlayer.kt:519-520
- Why it matters: persistentQueue defaults true and upstream persists the queue title, so after a restart the phone player still reads 'Playing from Infinity Mix' while infinityOn is false: thumbs revert to Like/Less, the why labels vanish and the title no longer opens the tuner. Desktop instead falls back to the song title, so the platforms disagree.
- Fix sketch: Persist the anchor (song id, title, source id) in turtle/bridge SqlitePlayEventStore and restore it in ShellQueue on first composition, or derive infinityOn from a Turtle-owned marker on the queue instead of a title string.

### [MAJOR] Which suggestion served each song is memory-only, so explanations and tags die with the process

- Evidence: turtle/bridge/.../PlayerObserver.kt:240-243 attribute() stores tags in memory; InfinityMix.kt:157 state.why is equally in-memory
- Why it matters: After a restart or a kill the restored queue plays on with no origin tags, so Smart Shuffle and Infinity songs are logged with no detail and the why labels are gone. The status doc already records the related symptom. This is the data Turtle's honesty claim rests on.
- Fix sketch: Persist the queue tag map and the why map keyed by queue occurrence in turtle/bridge/.../SqlitePlayEventStore.kt, and rehydrate them in PlayerObserver.startCheckpoints alongside the existing interrupted-play recovery.

### [MAJOR] Negative feedback has no undo anywhere, and the same screen's Like has none either

- Evidence: ShellPlayer.kt:570-573 Less like this toasts then skips; ShellPlayer.kt:574 Like on the phone full player also only toasts; ShellPlayerSheets.kt:443-456 the two blocks apply instantly
- Why it matters: A mis-tap on Less like this, a 48 dp target beside Like and Add to playlist, permanently downweights a song and skips it 250 ms later with no way back. The two blocks commit instantly and only point at Settings. Turtle already built the undo snackbar but wired it into the mini player and desktop bar, not here.
- Fix sketch: Give lessLikeThis and the two blocks the ShellSnackbar undo pattern from ShellPlayer.kt:420-427 with a TurtleBridge reversal call, and replace the phone full player's toast at :574 with rememberLikeSnackbar(song).

### [MAJOR] No song outside the Infinity Mix says why it is playing, although the origin is recorded

- Evidence: ShellInfinity.kt:269 returns early unless state.why holds the song id; SmartShuffleBridge.kt:79 writes PlayOrigin tags no UI reads; TurtleRuntime.rankRadio ranks radio pages with reasons
- Why it matters: Radio, autoplay and Smart Shuffle are where most listening happens, and they are silent. Explainability is the differentiator Turtle claims over a closed recommender, so the gap is in the one place the product stakes its advantage. The Spotify comparison credits Turtle with a why line as if it were general.
- Fix sketch: Promote the why map out of InfinityMix into a surface-agnostic lookup on TurtleBridge fed by PlayerObserver's PlayOrigin tags (QueueItem.reason exists at taste/Queue.kt:28), then render it from the existing InfinityWhyLabel call sites (ShellPlayer.kt:561, ShellPlayerSheets.kt:341, ShellDesktop.kt:682) without the infinityOn gate.

### [MAJOR] Smart Shuffle recommendations are indistinguishable from the playlist's own songs

- Evidence: SmartShuffleItem.isRecommendation (SmartShuffle.kt:30) and surfaceDetail (:31) have no reader in turtle/ui; QueueRow (ShellPlayerSheets.kt:299-352) shows title, artist and InfinityWhyLabel only
- Why it matters: A listener who asked for their playlist cannot see which songs are not in it, cannot tell a bad recommendation from a bad playlist choice, and has no per-song way to reject an insertion other than Less like this. This is the honest-labelling standard Turtle applies elsewhere not being applied here.
- Fix sketch: Pass the PlayOrigin tags SmartShuffleBridge.kt:79 already writes into a label slot on QueueRow (ShellPlayerSheets.kt:337) and under the player title, reusing IndexLabel as InfinityWhyLabel does.

### [MAJOR] Smart Shuffle degrades to a plain shuffle, or to a partial playlist, with nothing said to the listener

- Evidence: SmartShuffleBridge.kt:42-43 a 3 s timeout falls back to plain() and only logs; :47-49 any exception does the same; :66-68 when the rest of a paged playlist misses the 2 s budget only the loaded songs are shuffled, again only logged; ShellSmartShuffle.kt:40 drops taps while busy
- Why it matters: Three separate silent degradations. On a slow network the radar button behaves like plain Shuffle with no way to know, and on a long playlist the listener may be shuffling only the first page while believing the whole playlist is in play. A second tap during the 3 s window does nothing at all.
- Fix sketch: Return a result (mixed, plain, partial) from TurtleBridge.smartShuffle and surface it in ShellSmartShuffle.play as a ShellSnackbar with Retry; show a busy or disabled state on the button instead of swallowing the tap (ShellSmartShuffle.kt:40).

### [MAJOR] Smart Shuffle's recommendations cluster at the front of a long playlist and then stop

- Evidence: SmartShuffleBridge.kt:125-127 SEEDS = 3, RADIO_LIMIT = 50; SmartShuffle.kt:13 maxPerRecommendedArtist = 2; SmartShuffle.kt:110-120 interleave consumes picks in order and stops when they run out, with no refill
- Why it matters: The candidate pool is bounded (three radios, deduplicated, two per artist) while interleave wants one insertion per three playlist songs. On a 300-song playlist the inserts run out in roughly the first fifth and the rest is an ordinary shuffle, so the feature quietly stops working where a listener would notice a mix going stale.
- Fix sketch: Either scale SEEDS and RADIO_LIMIT with playlist length in SmartShuffleBridge.candidates, or make insertion lazy: keep the arranged head and refill candidates as playback advances, the way ShellInfinity.refill already does for the mix.

### [MINOR] Smart Shuffle ranks every candidate against a single seed

- Evidence: SmartShuffleBridge.kt:70 taste.rankExternal(seeds.first(), candidates); :60 picks three seeds by different artists so the mix 'spans the playlist'
- Why it matters: Candidates gathered to span the playlist are then scored as if they all came from one corner of it, so the ranking bias contradicts the seed strategy the same function sets up. TasteEngine.rankExternal takes a nullable seed (TasteEngine.kt:333-338), so it could rank per seed and merge.
- Fix sketch: Rank each seed's candidates against that seed and merge round-robin, as candidates() already does for fetching, in turtle/bridge/.../SmartShuffleBridge.kt:70.

### [MAJOR] Lyrics are never prefetched, so opening Lyrics always shows the skeleton first

- Evidence: ShellPlayerSheets.kt:600 ensureLyrics runs in a LaunchedEffect when the surface is first composed; PlayerScreen.kt:791-803 upstream's next-song prefetch sits after the Turtle slot's early return at PlayerScreen.kt:474
- Why it matters: Every skip into a new song and every open of the Lyrics surface starts a network fetch, so the first seconds have no lyrics. For a song with no lyrics the guard at ShellPlayer.kt:892-895 is never satisfied, so each reopen refetches the same empty result.
- Fix sketch: Add a LaunchedEffect in ShellNowPlaying calling vm.prefetchLyrics for queue index + 1 after about 30 s, mirroring PlayerScreen.kt:791, and remember a per-song 'no lyrics' result in ShellPlayerServices.ensureLyrics so it stops refetching.

### [MAJOR] Lyrics timing cannot be corrected at all: both offsets are unreachable

- Evidence: ShellPlayerSheets.kt:143 lyricsOffsetMs is read-only; PlayerViewModel.kt:1965 setLyricsOffset has no Turtle caller; LyricsSettingsRegistration.kt:90 globalLyricsOffset is filtered out by ShellSettingsTurtle.kt:209
- Why it matters: Community lyrics are often a few hundred milliseconds out, and Shell's word-level sweep makes that drift far more visible than a plain line highlight. The listener's only recourse is to stop using lyrics, even though upstream supports both a global and a per-song offset.
- Fix sketch: Add "globalLyricsOffset" to KEEP_LYRICS_BEHAVIOR (ShellSettingsTurtle.kt:209), and put a nudge control plus the per-song offset in a new overflow on the lyrics header (ShellPlayerSheets.kt:585 SurfaceHeader) wired to PlayerViewModel.setLyricsOffset.

### [MINOR] Lyrics failures and translation state are invisible, and Manual translation can never be triggered

- Evidence: PlayerViewModel.kt:191 lyricsTranslationFailures and uiState.lyricsTranslationProgress have no reader in turtle/; :1567 setManualLyricsTranslation has no Turtle caller; LyricsSettingsRegistration.kt:311 activationMode offers Manual
- Why it matters: AI translation fails silently on a credit limit or bad key, and with activation mode set to Manual the setting is reachable while the action is not, so translation can be configured and never happen. The empty-state hint names providers without offering the source list or a retry.
- Fix sketch: Collect lyricsTranslationFailures in ShellLyricsFor and show them in a ShellSnackbar; add a Translate toggle and Retry/Sources actions to the lyrics header; link shell_lyrics_none_hint to Settings > Lyrics > Sources.

### [MAJOR] The sleep timer shows no remaining time, offers no fade-out, and the two sleep sheets disagree

- Evidence: ShellQueue.kt:106 calm() strips remainingMs and no turtle/ui code reads it; ShellPlayer.kt:903 passes fadeOut = false; desktopApp/Main.kt:991 hard-codes fadeOut = false; ShellSpoken.kt:228-230 passes fadeOut = true
- Why it matters: Someone falling asleep cannot tell how long is left; the only feedback is 'Sleep timer is on'. Music cuts off abruptly because fade-out is never requested, while the podcast sheet does fade, so the same gesture behaves differently depending on what is playing.
- Fix sketch: Read controller.state.sleepTimerState.remainingMs in a leaf composable (the pattern ShellQueue.kt:113 documents), add a fade-out switch and a custom duration to ShellSleepTimer (ShellPlayerSheets.kt:460), and make ShellPlayer.kt:903, Main.kt:991 and ShellSpoken.kt:228 agree.

### [MAJOR] Neither the seek bar nor the volume slider can be used from the keyboard

- Evidence: ShellPlayer.kt:276-290 ShellSeekBar handles only hover, tap and horizontal drag, with no focusable() or onKeyEvent; ShellComponents.kt:101-125 shellPressable, which grants focus, is not applied to it; DesktopMenuBar.kt has no seek shortcut
- Why it matters: A desktop keyboard-only user cannot seek within a song or set the volume from the slider. Volume at least has menu shortcuts (DesktopMenuBar.kt:549-559); seek has none anywhere, so there is no keyboard path to a position in a track. The setProgress node at ShellPlayer.kt:326 serves assistive tech, not keyboards.
- Fix sketch: Add focusable() plus onKeyEvent arrow/Home/End handling to ShellSeekBar (ShellPlayer.kt:276) and draw the existing focus ring, and add seek-forward and seek-back items to DesktopMenuBar's Playback menu.

### [MINOR] Queue edits can fail silently, doing nothing and saying nothing

- Evidence: ShellPlayer.kt:916-919 keys() returns null when the view model's entryKeys and the controller's queue differ in size; ShellPlayerSheets.kt:204-211 remove() then returns false after already calling ShellSnackbar.dismiss()
- Why it matters: In that window the row's remove button and Clear queue do nothing with no message (the swipe path at least springs back, ShellPlayerSheets.kt:391), and any snackbar undo the listener was about to use has already been dismissed. The size check is a real guard, so the failure is reachable.
- Fix sketch: Have ShellQueueEditor report refusal and show a ShellSnackbar from remove() and commit() in ShellPlayerSheets.kt:204-220, and do not dismiss the previous snackbar until the edit is known to have applied.

### [MINOR] The discovery dial is shown for podcasts and audiobooks, where it means nothing

- Evidence: ShellPlayer.kt:606 SteerCard(tint) is called unconditionally in the phone player; ShellDesktop.kt:698 the same in the desktop panel; ShellSpoken.kt:111 shows the spoken-word controls right above it
- Why it matters: 'Steer your mix: 70% familiar / 30% new' sits under a book chapter or a podcast episode that the dial does not affect, and the percentages keep claiming a blend while an ordinary playlist plays. It teaches the listener the control is decorative, undercutting the feature the product is built around.
- Fix sketch: Gate SteerCard on !ShellSpoken.showsControls(song) in ShellPlayer.kt:606, and for non-Infinity queues drop the familiar/new captions by passing captions = false, as ShellDesktop.kt:698 already does.

### [MINOR] Queue identity is decided by comparing a localized display string

- Evidence: ShellPlayer.kt:520 and :773 compare ShellQueue.activeTitle(state) to stringResource(Res.string.shell_infinity_title); ShellDesktop.kt:684 the same; turtle/ui/.../values/strings.xml:7 defines it as "Infinity Mix"
- Why it matters: A user playlist named 'Infinity Mix' takes over the player: thumbs replace Like, the source name becomes a tuner and why labels appear for songs the mix never chose. The reverse happens if the app language changes mid-session, because the anchor holds the old localized title. Four call sites must agree on a translated string.
- Fix sketch: Store a stable kind on ShellQueue's Anchor (ShellQueue.kt:22) set by ShellInfinity.play, and have the call sites ask ShellQueue.isInfinity(state) instead of comparing strings.

### [MINOR] The queue is one flat list: no section for what you added, and no explicit label

- Evidence: ShellPlayerSheets.kt:183-184 splits only into played and upcoming and renders Now playing plus Up next; QueueRow (:299-352) shows no explicit tag, unlike ShellCollection.kt:1351 which does
- Why it matters: Songs the listener queued, the source's remaining tracks and autoplay's continuation look identical, so there is no way to see where a chosen queue ends and the machine's guesses begin, which matters more here because the continuation is Turtle's own. Explicit content is flagged on collection rows and in search but not in the queue or Now Playing.
- Fix sketch: Add section headings to ShellQueueList (upstream's QueueScreen.kt:929 QueueSectionHeading shows the pattern) keyed off the PlayOrigin tags PlayerObserver holds, and reuse ShellCollection.kt:1351's explicit tag in QueueRow and under the player title.

## Strengths

- **The discovery dial is a real, immediate, numeric control over the recommender** — ShellPlayer.kt:622-681 five stops with haptics and shared engine state; :657 a change re-mixes the queued songs; :663-677 prints the familiar/new split as percentages | beats Spotify: Turtle exposes the familiar-versus-new trade-off as a first-class control in the player, states the resulting share as a number, and applies it to already-queued songs mid-session, so the listener sets the exploration rate rather than inferring it.
- **The mix tells the listener the truth about its own output, including when it cannot deliver** — ShellPlayer.kt:707-714 prints the batch's actual familiar count out of its total; :702-706 says when the profile has too few favourites to hit the target (shell_inf_blend_sparse) | beats Spotify: Turtle separates the target blend from the blend it actually achieved, and admits a sparse profile instead of presenting a weaker result as the intended one.
- **Per-song feedback that is honest about its scope: a soft steer and a hard block** — ShellQueue.kt:172-184 lessLikeThis steers and skips; ShellPlayerSheets.kt:443-456 the two blocks, which SmartShuffle.kt:95-99 also honours | beats Spotify: Two clearly separated strengths of rejection, and the hard block is enforced inside Turtle's own ranking paths rather than being a hint passed to a remote model.
- **Queue editing with undo on both platforms, including one built over an API that has none** — ShellPlayer.kt:915-930 phone edits through upstream's entry keys and undo; ShellQueue.kt:80-101 ShellQueueRestore re-adds and reorders on desktop; ShellPlayerSheets.kt:204-211 one pending undo at a time | beats Spotify: Queue removals and Clear queue are reversible, and the reorder renders the order being built locally before the platform applies it (ShellPlayerSheets.kt:187-189) so the list never jumps.
- **Lyrics with word-level sync, romanization and translation, drawn without per-tick recomposition** — ShellLyrics.kt:443-502 the sweep is painted in the draw phase from a position lambda; :431-436 romanized and translated sub-lines; :363-371 background vocals light with their line | beats Spotify: Word-level timing, romanization and translation render together on one line, and the work stays in the draw phase, so the effect holds on a phone without dropping frames.
- **Lyrics behaviour respects the reader rather than the playhead** — ShellLyrics.kt:287-304 a user scroll pauses auto-follow for 4 s after the fling settles; :345-359 an explicit 'Back to current line' button; :337 tap a line to seek | beats Spotify: Reading ahead is not fought by the auto-scroll, and returning to the playhead is an explicit action rather than a timeout the listener cannot see.
- **Accessibility work that is unusual for a music player** — ShellPlayer.kt:312-329 the seek bar speaks elapsed and total and accepts setProgress; ShellPlayerSheets.kt:318-322 queue remove and move as screen-reader actions; ShellPlayer.kt:383-388 mini-player skip; :182-195 tint clamped for readability | beats Spotify: Every gesture-only interaction in the queue and mini player has a non-gesture equivalent, and the artwork tint is validated against the actual text colours drawn on it rather than assumed readable.
- **Smart Shuffle's arrangement rules are explicit, deterministic and testable** — SmartShuffle.kt:40-50 every playlist song exactly once, no artist twice in a row where avoidable, a slot left empty rather than breaking that rule; :124-152 shuffleSpaced proves feasibility before each pick; SmartShuffleTest.kt | beats Spotify: The shuffle's guarantees are stated and unit-tested, deduplicate the same song across different uploads (SmartShuffle.kt:163), and are pure for a given Random, so a complaint about repetition can be reproduced rather than guessed at.
- **Playback resumes after the system kills the app, but never after the listener stopped it** — CrashResume.kt:23-30 resumes only for CRASH or SYSTEM_KILL, within 30 s, and only when a 'was playing' mark was fresh; :57 waits for the queue to be restored before pressing play | beats Spotify: The distinction between the platform ending playback and the listener ending it is made explicitly, so recovery never turns into unwanted autoplay.
- **Queue rules that refuse to pad with repetition, and no paid placement anywhere** — taste/Queue.kt:68 'Rules are never relaxed: when nothing fits, the queue is shorter rather than repetitive'; :10-22 artist spacing, per-album-per-hour cap, 3 h repeat gap, 500-item recommendation memory | beats Spotify: Artist spacing, album caps and repeat gaps are hard constraints with no fallback that relaxes them, and the queue-building path contains no advertising, sponsored insertion or payola hook of any kind.
