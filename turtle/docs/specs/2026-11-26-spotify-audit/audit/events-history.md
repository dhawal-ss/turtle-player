## Weaknesses

### [BLOCKER] Turtle's whole database is excluded from backup and restore

- Evidence: turtle/bridge/src/androidMain/.../TurtleDatabase.android.kt:8 (turtle.db); shared/src/commonMain/kotlin/com/metrolist/shared/backup/BackupFormat.kt:23 and androidApp/.../backup/BackupRestoreManager.kt:156-176 zip only song.db, settings, manifest and artist aliases
- Why it matters: A listener who changes phone, reinstalls or restores a backup loses every play event, every feedback vote, the taste state, the discovery dial, the co-listening graph and the reset watermark. Spotify's profile follows the account; Turtle's follows nothing. Silent, total loss of the only data Turtle's product depends on.
- Fix sketch: Add turtle.db (plus -wal) as a new backup entry: extend BACKUP_* constants in shared/src/commonMain/kotlin/com/metrolist/shared/backup/BackupFormat.kt, write and restore it in androidApp/.../backup/BackupRestoreManager.kt and desktopApp/.../backup/DesktopBackupRestoreManager.kt, and close/reopen SqlitePlayEventStore around a restore. Gate on a manifest field so older backups still restore.

### [BLOCKER] Imported Spotify plays can never match a locally played song's track affinity

- Evidence: turtle/bridge/.../SongItemMapping.kt:15 keys every local play as yt:<id>; turtle/engines/events/.../imports/SpotifyHistoryImporter.kt:181 keys imports as meta:...; turtle/engines/taste/.../TasteProfile.kt:403 and :213 key per-track affinity on the raw TrackKey
- Why it matters: The import is sold as cold-start fuel, but a song played 400 times on Spotify gets a separate accumulator from the same song on YouTube. Track affinity, fatigue, spacing and "Because you replayed X" all miss it. Only artist affinity and the heard-set (TasteProfile.kt:507) merge. The comment at SpotifyHistoryImporter.kt:180 claims the keys merge; they do not.
- Fix sketch: Key the profile's track map on the SongKeys identity rather than the raw TrackKey in turtle/engines/taste/.../TasteProfile.kt (add/snapshot), keeping the raw key only for display metadata; or add a key-alias table in SqlitePlayEventStore that maps meta: keys to yt: ids as songs are matched, applied on read. Fix the misleading comment either way.

### [BLOCKER] Deleting a play in the History screen does not delete it from Turtle's log

- Evidence: shared/src/commonMain/kotlin/com/metrolist/shared/ui/mobile/MobileHistoryScreen.kt:147-161 deletes upstream Event rows; turtle/bridge/.../SqlitePlayEventStore.kt:296 is the only DELETE on play_event and is per-source; turtle/bridge/.../TurtleRuntime.kt:701 forbids LOCAL
- Why it matters: A listener who removes an embarrassing or mistaken play, including from their YouTube account, still has it shaping Turtle's recommendations forever, and no UI anywhere can remove it. There is also no retention cap, so play_event grows without bound. Both a trust break and a plausible data-rights problem for an app that advertises on-device honesty.
- Fix sketch: Add deleteByTrack/deleteRange to PlayEventStore and SqlitePlayEventStore, wire a "Remove from Turtle's history" path that mirrors upstream's delete (hook or observe deleteHistoryEntries), call historyReadingChanged() after, and expose a "Clear listening history" action in turtle/ui/.../ShellSettingsTurtle.kt beside Reset taste.

### [BLOCKER] No way for a listener to feed the Spotify or ListenBrainz importers

- Evidence: turtle/bridge/.../TurtleModule.kt:112-113 registers both importers; grep over turtle/ui finds no caller of commitHistoryImport or removeImportedHistory, and no ZipInputStream, ZipFile, FileDialog, OpenDocument or file picker anywhere under turtle/
- Why it matters: The most differentiated feature in this domain, importing years of Spotify history so Turtle is never cold, is fully built, fully tested and completely unreachable. Shell shows nothing. Spotify's own onboarding beats Turtle here by default, because Turtle starts from zero.
- Fix sketch: Add an ExportFile provider per platform: SAF OpenDocument + ZipInputStream in turtle/bridge/src/androidMain, a FileDialog + java.util.zip in turtle/bridge/src/desktopMain, counting uncompressed chars per entry against ImportLimits.maxFileChars. Add a Shell preview-and-confirm screen over ImportPreview that also confirms shrinksHistory, plus a row calling removeImportedHistory.

### [MAJOR] Skips made with Shell's player out of composition are recorded as UNKNOWN

- Evidence: turtle/bridge/.../PlayerObserver.kt:310-316 returns UNKNOWN when no controller is attached and the previous song is not in the last seen queue; the controller is only attached while ShellNowPlaying or the desktop bar is composed (turtle/ui/.../ShellPlayer.kt:493, 731)
- Why it matters: Android Auto, and any session that outlives the Activity, produce no negative signal at all: a next-button press becomes UNKNOWN, which the tracker deliberately never treats as a skip. Turtle's own default keeps playback alive after the task is cleared (TurtleDefaults.kt:18-20), making that state common rather than rare. Car listening teaches the ranker almost nothing.
- Fix sketch: Keep a queue snapshot in the bridge independent of Shell's composition: publish the queue and index from the playback service through a small hook (androidApp MusicService, desktop PlayerState) into PlaybackHints, so PlayerObserver.classify has a live queue without the UI. Alternatively record media-session next/previous commands as explicit StartReason signals.

### [MAJOR] Two divergent histories; the one the listener sees is not the one Turtle learns from

- Evidence: androidApp/.../playback/AndroidListeningHistory.kt:94 writes upstream Event rows at the user's historyDuration threshold; turtle/bridge/.../MetrolistHistoryImport.kt:17-19 states upstream keeps recording beside Turtle
- Why it matters: History and Stats in the nav show upstream's table; recommendations come from Turtle's. The counts differ, deletions diverge, and a bug in either is invisible from the other. The listener has no way to tell which history explains a recommendation, which undercuts the "why this song" story Turtle is built on.
- Fix sketch: Either feed upstream's history screens from Turtle's store through a bridge adapter (a read-only PlayEventStore-backed source for MobileHistoryScreen and HistoryStats), or build Shell's own history and stats pages over play_event and hide upstream's History and Stats nav items. Pick one; do not ship both tables as peers.

### [MAJOR] The one-shot Metrolist backfill is latched, so history that arrives later never reaches Turtle

- Evidence: turtle/bridge/.../MetrolistHistoryImport.kt:28 returns early when PREF_DONE is set, and :41 writes it unconditionally; shared/.../db/HistoryStore.kt:391 and shared/.../backup/ForeignLibraryImport.kt:581 write Event rows at any later time
- Why it matters: Signing in to YouTube Music, enabling ytmSync, or importing another player's backup after first launch all fill upstream's event table with years of plays that Turtle will never see. The listener does the migration Turtle asked for and the engine stays cold. The pref is also written even when the import failed or committed nothing.
- Fix sketch: In turtle/bridge/.../MetrolistHistoryImport.kt, replace the boolean latch with a high-water mark: store the newest imported timestamp, re-run when upstream's event table gains rows older than Turtle's first own play (watch invalidationTracker on the event table), and only advance the mark when report.committed is true.

### [MAJOR] Imports invent a 30-second msPlayed and nothing marks it as estimated

- Evidence: turtle/engines/events/.../imports/ListenBrainzHistoryImporter.kt:176 (msPlayed = durationMs ?: LISTEN_MS); turtle/engines/events/.../imports/PlayerHistoryImporter.kt:55 (msPlayed = played ?: LISTEN_MS)
- Why it matters: A scrobble only says a track was heard, yet the row claims exactly 30 s of listening, which is also the isListen and isQuickSkip boundary. Any minutes-listened figure, any completion-rate statistic and any future recap built on these rows is wrong, and no column distinguishes a measured play from a guessed one.
- Fix sketch: Add a nullable msPlayed (or an estimated flag) to PlayEvent and the play_event table in turtle/bridge/.../SqlitePlayEventStore.kt as schema v8, have the importers leave it null, and make completion/isListen/isFinished treat null as unknown rather than 30 s. Exclude estimated rows from any minutes figure.

### [MAJOR] Spotify Account rows inside the Extended span are dropped wholesale as duplicates

- Evidence: turtle/engines/events/.../imports/SpotifyHistoryImporter.kt:149 counts any Account row whose end time falls between extendedFirstEnd and extendedLastEnd as a duplicate
- Why it matters: The de-duplication uses one global span, not per-row matching. If the Extended export is missing a month in the middle, or the listener supplies only part of it, every Account row covering that gap is silently discarded as a duplicate. The preview reports them as duplicates, so nothing warns the listener that real plays were thrown away.
- Fix sketch: In turtle/engines/events/.../imports/SpotifyHistoryImporter.kt, match Account rows against the Extended RowKey set at minute precision (name, artist, endMs/MINUTE_MS) instead of the span, or compute covered intervals from the Extended rows themselves and only drop rows inside a covered interval.

### [MAJOR] A taste reset is silently undone by any later import of the same source

- Evidence: turtle/bridge/.../TasteHistoryLens.kt:82 sets ignoreThroughId = store.lastPlayId(); turtle/bridge/.../SqlitePlayEventStore.kt:292-303 deletes and re-inserts a source's rows, so they get fresh AUTOINCREMENT ids above that watermark
- Why it matters: After Reset taste, removing and re-adding a Spotify import (or re-importing a newer export) brings that entire source back into the profile with no warning and no UI indication that the reset no longer applies. The setting says "Recommendations start over"; the behaviour quietly reverts.
- Fix sketch: Record the reset as a timestamp alongside the id in turtle/engines/events/.../HistoryLens.kt (ignoreBeforeMs plus ignoreThroughId) and filter on both in read(); or keep a per-source reset watermark written in TasteHistoryLens.reset() and re-apply it in TurtleRuntime.commitHistoryImport after store.commit.

### [MAJOR] An in-app like never reaches taste unless the liked song is the one playing

- Evidence: shared/.../state/SongLikeState.kt:592-611, 263-267 keeps likes in its own settings snapshot and never writes song.liked; turtle/bridge/.../LibrarySync.kt:71 only yields SavedKind.LIKED when song.liked is true; turtle/bridge/.../TurtleRuntime.kt:461-469 applies a like only to observer.currentSong()
- Why it matters: Liking from a list, from search, from the liked-songs screen or from history produces no taste signal at all: SavedKind.LIKED is dead, so taste.setSavedTracks (TurtleRuntime.kt:201) never sees likes. The like button is the single strongest explicit signal a listener gives, and most of the time Turtle discards it.
- Fix sketch: Feed SongLikeState's liked set directly into SavedTrack building in turtle/bridge/.../LibrarySync.kt (kind LIKED, atMs from the like snapshot) rather than relying on song.liked, and handle unlikes by diffing the set. Needs like times and removals, not only the newLikes stream.

### [MAJOR] Every taste rebuild materialises up to two years of plays with no aggregate path

- Evidence: turtle/engines/taste/.../TasteEngine.kt:39 (historyDays = 730) and :789 (events.since(now - 730 days) with the default Int.MAX_VALUE limit); turtle/bridge/.../SqlitePlayEventStore.kt:312-317 returns fully built PlayEvent objects
- Why it matters: Cold start, every history change and every import commit load the whole window into a List<PlayEvent>. A heavy listener's two-year Spotify import is easily six figures of rows with strings per row, on a phone, on the path that gates Home's first paint. Nothing caps or pages this, and no aggregate query exists.
- Fix sketch: Add aggregate reads to SqlitePlayEventStore (per-track and per-artist decayed sums, per-context sums) and have TasteProfileBuilder consume them for rows older than a short recent window; or stream since() through a callback instead of returning a list, and cap imported rows per track.

### [MAJOR] Opening the recommendation settings loads every local play into memory

- Evidence: turtle/ui/.../ShellSettingsTurtle.kt:374 calls loadTasteRepair on composition; turtle/bridge/.../TasteHistoryLens.kt:56-59 calls HistoryLens.legacySkipCount(store.localPlays()); turtle/bridge/.../SqlitePlayEventStore.kt:342-346 selects every LOCAL row with no limit
- Why it matters: A UI-triggered path reads the entire local play table and builds three hash maps over it (HistoryLens.kt:69-86) just to render one subtitle number. On a long-lived install that is a visible stall or an OOM risk on a settings screen, for information that never changes after the last pre-versioning play.
- Fix sketch: Compute the legacy-skip count once in SqlitePlayEventStore with a SQL query over label_version IS NULL AND end_reason = 'SKIPPED' and cache it in the pref table, or compute it on the background scope at startup and publish the number rather than recomputing on every settings open.

### [MAJOR] The whole outcome-measurement layer is write-only in shipped builds

- Evidence: turtle/bridge/.../SaveWatcher.kt:86-122 writes PREF_MEASURE_SINCE and SAVED feedback rows; turtle/engines/taste/.../eval/ListenerOutcomes.kt and eval/OriginReport.kt have no production caller (only tests and turtle/bridge/src/desktopTest/.../LocalOriginReportProbe.kt)
- Why it matters: Turtle records every like and library add with its timestamp, forever, for a save-or-replay measurement nobody can read without a Gradle test flag. That is data collected without a purpose the listener benefits from, which contradicts the data-minimisation stance the importers are so careful about, and nobody can tell whether discovery works.
- Fix sketch: Either surface the reports (a Turtle settings "How discovery is doing" page over ListenerOutcomes, which also answers the missing recap capability), or stop writing SAVED rows until a reader ships and drop the table's rows on upgrade.

### [MAJOR] A bulk library import records thousands of separate SAVED likes at import time

- Evidence: shared/.../state/SongLikeState.kt:615-627 emits newLikes for every song of addLikes, which ForeignLibraryImport.kt:544, 658 calls; turtle/bridge/.../SaveWatcher.kt:90-91 records each one immediately with nowMs()
- Why it matters: Library adds go through the settle and burst rules (SaveWatcher.kt:95-116), but likes do not. Importing a Spotify or Metrolist library writes one SAVED row per liked song, all stamped with the import moment, which corrupts the save-rate denominator the discovery measurement is built on and inflates the feedback table.
- Fix sketch: Route likes through the same PlaylistAdds settle/burst path in turtle/bridge/.../SaveWatcher.kt (a pseudo-playlist id for likes), or have the bridge call PlaylistAdds.expectBulk around any import that emits likes.

### [MAJOR] No negative feedback kind for unlike, playlist removal or library removal

- Evidence: turtle/engines/events/.../TrackFeedback.kt:10 defines only ADDED_TO_PLAYLIST, NOT_INTERESTED, UNDO_NOT_INTERESTED, SAVED; turtle/engines/taste/.../TasteProfile.kt:386-389 adds affinity for an add and has no inverse
- Why it matters: Taking a song out of a playlist or out of the library is a clear retraction, and Turtle ignores it: the original add keeps raising affinity for both the track and the artist for as long as the decay window lasts. Spotify at least stops counting a removed save. Only the play-level UNLIKED signal exists, and only for the song playing now.
- Fix sketch: Add REMOVED_FROM_PLAYLIST, REMOVED_FROM_LIBRARY and UNLIKED kinds in TrackFeedback.kt (append only), detect removals by diffing the same snapshots PlaylistAdds and SaveWatcher already take, and subtract the matching weight in TasteProfile.addFeedback.

### [MINOR] Four stored signals are dead: seeks, shuffle, device and network

- Evidence: turtle/bridge/.../PlayerObserver.kt:374-383 builds TrackStarted without shuffle (default null, ListeningSessionTracker.kt:17); ContextChanged has no emitter; .seeks has no reader in turtle/engines outside the tracker
- Why it matters: Four columns are written on every row and every import for nothing. shuffle is only ever non-null for Spotify imports, so the ranker cannot tell a deliberate pick from a shuffled one; seeks is captured but never read; device and network are always UNKNOWN, making Feature.DEVICE (Features.kt:173) a constant input to the ranker.
- Fix sketch: Either emit them (pass the controller's shuffle state in PlayerObserver.start, emit ContextChanged from the Android connectivity and media-route state and the desktop equivalent) or delete the fields and columns in a schema v8 so nothing reads a constant as a feature.

### [MINOR] Turtle hardcodes 30 seconds and ignores the listener's own history threshold

- Evidence: shared/.../ui/settings/PrivacySettingsRegistration.kt:88-97 and shared/.../settings/Settings.kt:564 expose historyDuration (5-60 s, default 30); turtle/engines/events/.../PlayEvent.kt:110-111 fixes QUICK_SKIP_MS and LISTEN_MS at 30 s
- Why it matters: A listener who moves the slider to 10 s, in a Shell-rendered settings page Turtle itself annotates, changes upstream's history but not what Turtle counts as a listen or a quick skip. The setting appears to control recording and silently half-does.
- Fix sketch: Pass Settings.shared.historyDuration into ListeningSessionTracker and PlayEvent's thresholds through TurtleModule.kt:105-110 as a supplier, or state in turtle/ui/.../ShellSettingsTurtle.kt's pauseHistoryBody-style description that the slider does not affect Turtle's own log.

### [MINOR] A short track skipped backwards counts as both finished and quick-skipped

- Evidence: turtle/engines/events/.../PlayEvent.kt:100-104 (isQuickSkip = any skip under 30 s; isFinished excludes only SKIPPED, not SKIPPED_BACK); turtle/engines/taste/.../TasteProfile.kt:408-413 increments t.finishes and t.quickSkips independently
- Why it matters: On a track under about 37 s, a back-button press near the end makes completion >= 0.8 while msPlayed < 30 s, so the same play is counted as a finish and as a quick skip in the per-track and per-artist stats and in the global finished count. Interludes, skits and short loops trip this exactly.
- Fix sketch: Exclude EndReason.isSkip (not just SKIPPED) from isFinished in turtle/engines/events/.../PlayEvent.kt:103-104, and make isQuickSkip require completion below the finish threshold so the two are mutually exclusive.

### [MINOR] The crash checkpoint can be wiped before recovery reads it, and is lost if the append fails

- Evidence: turtle/bridge/.../PlayerObserver.kt:248-256 launches recover() in the same start() as attach; ListeningSessionTracker.kt:138 calls checkpoint() after every close and SqlitePlayEventStore.kt:264 begins save() with DELETE FROM open_play; :269-273 take() deletes before append runs
- Why it matters: A song change that closes a play before recover() wins the race replaces the saved crash checkpoint, losing the interrupted play. Separately, take() clears the row first, so a failing append (locked database, disk error) loses it with no retry. Each loss is one play, but it is the play the checkpoint exists to save.
- Fix sketch: Await recover() before attaching the song-change collector in turtle/bridge/.../PlayerObserver.kt (gate attach on a CompletableDeferred completed by startCheckpoints), and in SqlitePlayEventStore make take() read without deleting, with an explicit clear after the append succeeds.

## Strengths

- **Reset and repair change only how history is read, never the history** — turtle/engines/events/.../HistoryLens.kt:17-38, 95-109; turtle/bridge/.../TasteHistoryLens.kt:62-98; turtle/ui/.../composeResources/values/strings_settings.xml:98-103 | beats Spotify: Spotify offers no way to reset recommendations at all. - **The importer parses only what taste needs and nothing identifying** — turtle/engines/events/.../imports/SpotifyHistoryImporter.kt:19-33, 60-62 | beats Spotify: Track URIs, platform, country, IP address and offline flags are deliberately not in the data class, so they are never decoded or stored, and only files the listener hands over are read. - **Input bounds mean a hostile or wrong export cannot exhaust memory** — turtle/engines/events/.../imports/HistoryImport.kt:32-52, 179-207 | beats Spotify: File count, per-file chars, total chars and row count are all capped, a zip entry's declared size is re-checked against the text actually read, and a file over a limit fails the import rather than being decoded.
- **Bulk actions are not mistaken for individual votes** — turtle/bridge/.../PlaylistAdds.kt:42-110 | beats Spotify: A playlist that takes more than 50 new songs in one burst is read as an import or a sync and dropped whole; a Turtle mix saved in one tap counts as one action for ten minutes; re-adds and reorder churn never count twice.

- **The play in progress survives the app being killed** — turtle/engines/events/.../ListeningSessionTracker.kt:142-176; turtle/bridge/.../SqlitePlayEventStore.kt:263-273; turtle/bridge/.../PlayerObserver.kt:248-256 | beats Spotify: A checkpoint rewritten every ten seconds is reconciled at the next launch as COMPLETED or STOPPED, so a crash during a long listen still teaches the ranker instead of vanishing.
