## Weaknesses

### [MAJOR] Shell's sleep timer cuts the audio dead mid-song: no fade, never finishes the track

- Evidence: ShellPlayer.kt:903 passes fadeOut=false and omits finishCurrentSong; desktopApp/Main.kt:991 (hook #30) also passes fadeOut=false. Upstream's own dialog offers both (PlayerScreen.kt:2520, Player.kt:255; PlaybackController.kt:147-151)
- Why it matters: The sleep timer is used at night, in bed. Upstream and Spotify both let the current track finish or fade; Turtle stops it abruptly at an arbitrary point, which is exactly the moment a listener notices. The 'end of song' row is a separate choice, so the timed rows are always abrupt.
- Fix sketch: In turtle/ui/.../ShellPlayer.kt:903 pass fadeOut=true and finishCurrentSong from a new toggle in ShellPlayerSheets.ShellSleepTimer (:459-470). Change the desktopExtras fadeOut to true in desktopApp/.../Main.kt:991 (hook #30, same line, no budget change). Align ShellSpoken.startSleep (:216-227).

### [MAJOR] Volume normalization is off by default, so Turtle's own mixes jump in loudness

- Evidence: shared/.../settings/Settings.kt:424 `var audioNormalization by preference(false)`. Turtle sets no default for it: TurtleDefaults.kt:21 lists only keepPlayingOnTaskClear
- Why it matters: Turtle's product is mixes that stitch together arbitrary YouTube uploads - a 2014 remaster next to a loud 2024 single next to a quiet live upload. Spotify normalizes by default. An unnormalized Daily Mix makes Turtle sound broken next to Spotify through the same headphones.
- Fix sketch: Add a Default to turtle/bridge/.../TurtleDefaults.kt (key `default_audio_normalization_v1`) setting Settings.shared.audioNormalization = true and loudnessLevel = LoudnessLevel.BALANCED, then append it to the `all` list at :21. Zero upstream lines; the existing applyOnce machinery and TurtleDefaultsTest cover it.

### [MAJOR] Android listeners have no way to change music playback speed

- Evidence: playbackSpeed is registered only for DESKTOP (PlaybackSettingsRegistration.kt:175-190). The Android UI was PlayerMenu.kt:217,275, reached only from PlayerScreen.kt:2497, which Shell never shows (ShellPlayer.kt:515). ShellSpoken's SpeedPill self-gates (ShellSpoken.kt:112)
- Why it matters: Speed control is a standard phone-player expectation and Metrolist shipped it. Under Shell it silently vanished on the platform Turtle actually releases (the Android APK). A listener who used it in Metrolist and migrates loses it with no explanation.
- Fix sketch: Either widen ShellSpoken.showsControls (turtle/ui/.../ShellSpoken.kt:112) to any song and reuse the existing SpeedPill, or add a speed row to ShellPlayerSheets.ShellPlayerMore (:426-456) calling controller.setPlaybackSpeed. No hook lines; strings already exist (shell_sp_speed, shell_sp_speed_value).

### [MAJOR] Android Auto shows YouTube Music's raw home, not a single Turtle shelf

- Evidence: AutomotiveMediaLibrary.kt:426-430 calls repository.getHomePageData().sections directly, bypassing HomeShelfBuilder.kt:484 where hook #21 injects Turtle's shelves. Root folders (:483-536) list Library/Home/History/Liked/Top - no mixes, no Infinity
- Why it matters: Driving is a peak listening context and the car is where a recommendation engine earns its keep. Spotify's Android Auto surface leads with Made For You. Turtle's car browse tree is indistinguishable from stock Metrolist, so none of the taste work reaches the moment it matters most.
- Fix sketch: Add a bridge seam (e.g. TurtleBridge.autoShelves()) in turtle/bridge, then one hook line in androidApp/.../playback/AutomotiveMediaLibrary.kt homeChildren (around :426) and one folder row in allRootChildren (:518) for Infinity. ~2 hook lines; 4 remain under the budget of 90.

### [MAJOR] Turtle's taste feedback exists nowhere outside the app's own screens

- Evidence: MusicService.kt:2112-2180 builds like, shuffle, repeat, add-to-playlist and radio CommandButtons only. 'Never play this song' and the discovery dial live solely in ShellPlayerSheets.kt:443-456 and ShellSettingsTurtle.kt:460-466
- Why it matters: Most skips happen on the lock screen, in the notification, in the car and on the widget. Turtle learns from skips but can never be told why, so the one control that distinguishes it from Spotify ('never play this again, including other uploads') is unavailable precisely when a listener wants it.
- Fix sketch: Add a Not-interested CommandButton beside createLikeButton in androidApp/.../playback/MusicService.kt:2112 whose session command calls into TurtleBridge (SongLikeState.dislike plus Turtle's exclusion). One hook line plus bridge code; needs owner approval for the hook budget.

### [MAJOR] Playback cannot be handed to another device from the player; Device Connect is buried in Integrations

- Evidence: Shell's Devices sheet lists only Cast devices (ShellPlayerSheets.kt:474-505). Hook #28 supplies onToggleDevices (TurtleUiSlots.kt:180) but turtle/ui never calls it - the only reference is the test fake ShellFakes.kt:284. The deviceconnect/ tree is referenced nowhere in turtle/
- Why it matters: 'Transfer playback to another device' is Spotify Connect's signature affordance and the thing people compare first. Turtle has the whole protocol (pairing, crypto, chunked playback, auto-reconnect) and exposes it only as a settings list reached through an account menu or an Integrations page.
- Fix sketch: Extend ShellDevices (turtle/ui/.../ShellPlayerSheets.kt:474) to also render upstream's ConnectedDevicesContent (shared/ui/components/ConnectedDevicesContent.kt:78), or at minimum call slot.onToggleDevices on desktop and platform.DeviceConnectSheet on Android. No new hook lines on desktop; Android needs a slot argument.

### [MAJOR] Turtle's widgets, update notifications and restore screen still say "Metrolist"

- Evidence: androidApp/build.gradle.kts:390 points generateAndroidStrings at the RAW shared/src/commonMain/composeResources, not at hook #44's filtered output (shared/build.gradle.kts:490-496). Leaking keys: tap_to_play, updates_channel_description, restore_in_progress, discord_button2_text_default

Build-file change, no hook lines.

### [MAJOR] Turtle claims metrolist.cc App Links and the metrolist:// schemes it cannot own

- Evidence: AndroidManifest.xml:133-147 declares autoVerify for https://metrolist.cc paths; :197 claims scheme `metrolist`; :204 claims `metrolist-auth` for the Discord callback. The app id is app.turtleplayer (turtle/release/identity.android.gradle via hook #63)
- Why it matters: metrolist.cc's assetlinks.json will not list Turtle's package and signing cert, so Android 12+ leaves the filter unverified and those links will not open Turtle. The custom schemes collide with an installed Metrolist, which Turtle explicitly installs beside (.github/README.md:20). Discord OAuth callbacks can land in the wrong app.
- Fix sketch: Replace the metrolist.cc host block with Turtle's own domain (or drop it), and rename the schemes to turtle / turtle-auth in androidApp/src/main/AndroidManifest.xml, desktopApp WindowsAppIdentity.kt and DesktopDiscordRegistration.kt. HOOKS.md's deferred table already scopes this; needs owner sign-off on the hook budget.

### [MAJOR] No in-app updater screen under Shell, although the updaters were repointed at Turtle

- Evidence: Hooks #64/#65 changed AppUpdateManager.RELEASES_URL and DesktopUpdateManager.API_BASE, but Shell reuses SettingsPageId.UPDATES for its Advanced page (ShellSettingsTurtle.kt:622-624), so upstream's UpdatesScreen.kt:160-206 and UPDATE_DETAILS are never rendered
- Why it matters: checkForUpdates defaults true (Settings.kt:597) and MetrolistApp.kt:636 checks at every startup, so listeners get prompts they cannot switch off, cannot trigger manually, and cannot read release notes for. For a 0.1.0 pilot shipping as an APK and a portable zip, the update path is the riskiest surface and it has no UI.
- Fix sketch: Give the Advanced page (turtle/ui/.../ShellSettingsTurtle.kt:622) its own id and add a navigationItem to SettingsPageId.UPDATES, or add raw[SettingsPageId.UPDATES] to the pages list at :709 under a Turtle title. Registry-only change, no hook lines.

### [MAJOR] Listening-history import has finished engines and no way to run it

- Evidence: turtle/engines/events/.../imports/ holds four importers and turtle/bridge/.../TurtleRuntime.kt:688-700 commits atomically, but turtle/bridge/src/androidMain contains only DriveLog.android.kt, TurtleDatabase.android.kt and CrashResume.android.kt - no SAF picker - and turtle/ui has no import screen
- Why it matters: Importing a Spotify extended-history export is the strongest migration story: a new listener gets good recommendations on day one instead of after weeks. Today a fresh install relies on up-to-five artist picks, and mix pages honestly admit 'All new to you'. The code is paid for and earns nothing.
- Fix sketch: Add expect/actual file reading in turtle/bridge (androidMain: ActivityResultContracts.OpenDocument + ZipInputStream counting chars against ImportLimits.maxFileChars; desktopMain: a file dialog) and a stage/preview/confirm screen in turtle/ui beside ShellSettingsTurtle's Recommendations page.

### [MAJOR] Android TV ships but Shell has no TV layout, focus order or initial focus

- Evidence: LEANBACK_LAUNCHER on every launcher alias (AndroidManifest.xml:241,259,277,295); upstream has shared/ui/tv/TvFocus.kt and 103 LocalIsTv call sites in shared/ and androidApp/. turtle/ui references LocalIsTv once (ShellSettings.kt:83,630) and TvFocus never. Hook #39 keeps Shell's Home on TV
- Why it matters: A TV install launches into Shell, which was designed for touch and mouse. shellPressable draws a focus border (ShellComponents.kt:117) but nothing requests initial focus or restores it, so a remote user may land on a screen with no focused element and no way in. Upstream's TV affordances are all bypassed.
- Fix sketch: Either declare TV unsupported (drop LEANBACK_LAUNCHER from androidApp/src/main/AndroidManifest.xml) or gate the slots in turtle/ui/.../TurtleUi.kt:28-33 on LocalIsTv so upstream's TV screens run. The second needs no hook lines and is the honest short-term answer.

### [MAJOR] Desktop has no OS media session: media keys only work while the window is focused

- Evidence: Media-key handling is in-app only and focus-gated (DesktopShortcuts.kt:261-277, :310, :451-455). No MPRIS, SystemMediaTransportControls or MediaRemote code exists. nucleus-app.properties:1 only sets an AUMID used for a registry key (WindowsAppIdentity.kt:13-27)
- Why it matters: On Windows the Turtle desktop build never appears in the system media flyout, keyboard media keys do nothing while another app has focus, and Linux desktop widgets see nothing. Spotify's desktop client registers with all three OS surfaces. Turtle's Windows zip is one of only two shipped artifacts.
- Fix sketch: Add a desktop-only bridge component in turtle/bridge/src/desktopMain registering SMTC on Windows (via JNA or the nucleus launcher) and MPRIS on Linux over DBus, driven by PlaybackController state. New files only, no hook lines; HOOKS.md #66's 'media controls' wording should be corrected either way.

### [MAJOR] Music videos cannot be watched or switched off anywhere under Shell

- Evidence: musicVideoPlaybackEnabled and musicVideoQuality are registered inside the PLAYER_STYLE page (AppearanceSettingsRegistration.kt:628-645, videoPlaybackSettings :1002-1037) whose id Shell reuses for Infinity (ShellSettingsTurtle.kt:206-209). turtle/ui has no video surface
- Why it matters: Metrolist's biggest advantage over Spotify is YouTube's video catalogue, and Shell removed it entirely: no video button, no viewer, no quality choice, no off switch. musicVideoPlaybackEnabled still defaults true (Settings.kt:725) and gates Canvas sources (CanvasLayers.kt:91), so a dead setting silently shapes other behaviour.
- Fix sketch: Decide explicitly. To keep video: add a video surface to turtle/ui/.../ShellPlayer.kt driving Settings.videoViewerVisible plus a raw.pick of musicVideoPlaybackEnabled/musicVideoQuality into Shell's Playback page. To drop it: set musicVideoPlaybackEnabled=false once via TurtleDefaults.kt and say so in the docs.

### [MAJOR] Nothing in the app adapts to metered connections

- Evidence: The only metered-aware pref is disableAnimatedArtworkOnMobileData (Settings.kt:711-714), itself on the unreachable PLAYER_ARTWORK page. No isActiveNetworkMetered/NET_CAPABILITY_NOT_METERED check outside ListenTogetherDebugReport.kt:99. audioQuality is one global value (Settings.kt:423)
- Why it matters: Behaviour patches #15 and #18 now fetch whole tracks (up to 32 MB) in the background on Android audio, which is excellent on Wi-Fi and expensive on a capped plan. Spotify documents a data saver and separate cellular quality. A listener on mobile data has no lever at all.
- Fix sketch: Add a Turtle data-saver preference in turtle/bridge, keyed off the existing PlaybackNetworkStateStore (used in androidApp/App.kt:412), that lowers audioQuality and switches off whole-track prefetch while metered, plus a row on Shell's Playback page. Bridge + Shell only; the prefetch flag already exists in HttpRangeInputStream.

### [BLOCKER] Car playback reportedly stops when the phone locks over Bluetooth and USB

- Evidence: Owner report in turtle/docs/plans/2026-11-23-status.md:105: playback 'can stop when the phone locks in the owner's car over Bluetooth and USB', cause unconfirmed. Instrumentation exists: turtle/bridge/.../DriveLog.kt:20 writes files/turtle-drive.log
- Why it matters: If real, listening simply stops in the car. ## Strengths

- **Sleep timer and spoken-word controls are first-class in the player, not buried in settings** — turtle/ui/.../ShellPlayerSheets.kt:441,459-470 (timer in the player's More sheet); ShellSpoken.kt:243-300 puts back-15 / speed / forward-30 / sleep directly on the player for podcasts and books | beats Spotify: The speed control is scoped to spoken word and resets music to 1x automatically (ShellSpoken.kt:167-180), so a podcast speed never leaks into the next song - a confusion a single global speed setting creates.
- **Turtle's exclusions hold on every radio path, ranked or not** — Hooks #10, #11 and #61 (turtle/HOOKS.md) route every radio page through TurtleRuntime.rankRadio, which drops Not-interested songs and their other uploads, muted artists and duplicate uploads even when ranking fails or times out; patch #62 fixes the end-of-queue stall | beats Spotify: 'Never play this song' is permanent and also covers the song's other uploads, so the same track re-uploaded under another video id stays out. A hide gesture that actually sticks is rare in streaming apps.
- **Playback starts and skips faster than stock Metrolist** — turtle/HOOKS.md behavior patches #13-#19: whole-track background fetch for audio up to 32 MB (HttpRangeInputStream.kt), prefetchAhead on Android direct audio, bufferForPlaybackMs 2000 to 750, next-track preload at 1.5 s instead of 5-30 s, 5 s ExoPlayer PreloadConfiguration | beats Spotify: Backward seeks, replays and skip-backs are served from memory rather than a fresh network request, which matters on a YouTube backend with no CDN control.
- **Playback position is checkpointed every 5 s, so a killed app resumes where it stopped** — Hook #60 (turtle/HOOKS.md) changes PLAYBACK_CHECKPOINT_INTERVAL_MS in shared/playback/PlaybackCheckpoints.kt from 30 s to 5 s, found on device when a OnePlus killed the app before its save-on-task-removal | beats Spotify: Diagnosed from a real device failure rather than assumed, and the cost (a few dozen bytes per checkpoint) is stated in the ledger.
- **Upstream defaults are changed once, reversibly, with no upstream edit** — turtle/bridge/.../TurtleDefaults.kt:10-45: each default has a key remembered in Turtle's own database, is applied once from TurtleRuntime.kt:130, publishes a snapshot notification, logs and skips failures, and never overrides a later user change
- **Every override fails open to upstream playback** — turtle/HOOKS.md: 'Every hook fails open: if Koin has no Turtle runtime, our code throws, or ranking misses its 1.5-second budget, upstream's own behavior runs unchanged.' Ranking keeps upstream's order when late but still applies exclusions
- **Listening history and the taste model stay on the device** — turtle/AGENTS.md ('Listening history stays on device. Anything sent off-device is opt-in and documented'); engines are pure commonMain with no network (turtle/engines/*); scrobbling and Discord are opt-in Integrations the listener enables | beats Spotify: Recommendations are computed locally from a local event log, and Turtle carries no advertising and no promotional-placement program of the kind Spotify documents for its catalogue.
- **Mix descriptions state the real share of songs the listener knows** — Committed at 09ae8be7: MixAbout counts known songs (FAMILIAR slot or TasteProfile.hasHeard) over the songs the page lists and says 'All new to you' / 'You know N% of these songs'; observed on device as 'You know 4% of these songs' (1 of 24) | beats Spotify: Spotify's shelf subtitles describe intent ('Non-stop music based on your favorite songs'). Turtle reports the measured composition of the mix it is about to play, which a listener can check.
- **Equalizer, crossfade, normalization and silence skipping are all available with no tier or paywall** — All on one page a Shell listener can reach: PlaybackSettingsRegistration.kt:214-279 (normalization, loudness target, equalizer link, crossfade, gapless exception) and :93-117 (skip silence), kept whole at ShellSettingsTurtle.kt:539 | beats Spotify: Spotify gates several playback settings behind Premium and ships its equalizer on mobile only; Turtle's full set is on both shipped platforms for everyone.
