## Weaknesses

### [BLOCKER] Weekly discovery can only ever propose songs the device has already seen on a radio page

- Evidence: Vector branch retrieves from the co-listening snapshot only (TasteEngine.kt:1070-1072, Generators.kt:95-122); the graph holds <=2000 artists x <=6 songs (CoListening.kt:22-26); CF falls back to CatalogWithObserved, also graph-bounded (:269-280)
- Why it matters: Spotify's Discover Weekly retrieves across the whole catalog. Turtle's pool is at most a few thousand songs, all previously fetched as radio pages, and it stops growing when history is paused (CoListeningSync.kt:39-41). A new listener gets a thin, repetitive shelf that re-proposes neighbours of what they already play.
- Fix sketch: Add a catalog-wide retrieval seam beside the graph: a bridge generator widening the pool from YouTube search or browse for the cluster top artists (new file beside turtle/bridge/.../YouTubeSeams.kt), registered in discoveryCollector (TasteEngine.kt:117); and/or wire EmbeddingPackReader (EmbeddingPack.kt:95-145) into TurtleModule.kt:124 behind a setting.

### [MAJOR] A dismissed mix is still built and keeps its slot for the whole day or week

- Evidence: suppressedReasons is read only in Generators.kt:352 and TasteEngine.kt:557; artistMixPlanLocked (:952-953), clusterMixesLocked (:999-1015) and moreLikePickLocked (:1023-1042) never consult it; stableLocked keeps the build (:584) and visibleLocked hides it after (:556-557)
- Why it matters: "Not interested in this mix" should give the listener a different mix. Instead Home silently loses a row: two mixes instead of three, or no More like row at all, and the same pick wins again tomorrow, so the gap persists for days. The listener's only whole-shelf feedback action makes Home emptier rather than better.
- Fix sketch: Pass controls.suppressedReasons into the planners: skip suppressed artists in topArtistSeedsLocked (TasteEngine.kt:936-949), skip suppressed clusters in clusterMixesLocked, and in moreLikePickLocked drop candidates whose Reason.key is suppressed before choosing, so the next-best seed takes the slot.

### [MAJOR] Dismissing "Because you replayed X" also removes X itself from mixes and Infinity's picks

- Evidence: CandidateCollector drops candidates whose reason key is suppressed (Generators.kt:352); TasteClusterGenerator:111-116 and FavoritesGenerator:138-143 give X's own candidate reason BecauseYouReplayed(X), key "replayed:X", exactly what ForReason stores (UserControls.kt:89)
- Why it matters: The listener dismisses a recommendation row and quietly loses one of their own favourite songs from every surface that goes through the generators: Daily Mixes, Weekly discovery and Infinity's for-you picks. Nothing in the UI says a song was affected. Radio still plays it, so the loss is invisible and inconsistent.
- Fix sketch: Give shelf dismissal its own namespace: in TasteEngine.lessLikeThis (:443-450) store a shelf-scoped key (e.g. "shelf:" + reason.key) and have visibleLocked (:557) match that, leaving CandidateCollector:352 to item-level reasons only; or exempt candidates whose reason track is the suppressed seed.

### [MAJOR] No rotation mechanism: a mix changes only as the profile moves

- Evidence: compose passes an empty play history and RecentlyRecommended(1), so neither the repeat gap nor the recent memory applies (TasteEngine.kt:907-910); the slot plan is a fixed share, not sampled (:909); ranking is a deterministic sort (:890); clustering seed is fixed (TasteProfile.kt:243)
- Why it matters: Spotify's Daily Mixes visibly change every day. Turtle rebuilds at local midnight and gets almost the same 30 songs in almost the same order, so "Refreshes daily" (strings_home.xml:36) promises something the listener does not see. A listener who did not like today's mix gets it again tomorrow.
- Fix sketch: Seed per-period variation: pass the period (TasteEngine.periodOf, :566-569) into compose as a rotation offset, sample the familiar share with policy.sampleShare as rerankLocked does (:876), and feed the engine's recent memory into compose instead of RecentlyRecommended(1) so yesterday's picks are deprioritised.

### [MAJOR] No cross-shelf dedup: one Home build can show the same song in several mixes

- Evidence: Only artistMixesLocked shares a used set (TasteEngine.kt:968,994); clusterMixesLocked (:1003-1014), weeklyDiscoveryLocked (:1079-1091), freshForYouLocked (:1121-1127) and rediscoverLocked (:1129-1134) each call compose with its own fresh RecentlyRecommended(1) (:909)
- Why it matters: Home can open with the same new song in Weekly discovery, Fresh for you and a Daily Mix, and the same favourite in On repeat, Rediscover and a cluster mix. Each row's distinct promise is undermined, and the listener sees less variety than the engine actually found.
- Fix sketch: Thread one used-key set through homeShelves (TasteEngine.kt:524-535) in build order and pass it to compose (:898-911) as an extra exclude, or build one shared RecentlyRecommended for the whole refresh instead of a per-shelf RecentlyRecommended(1).

### [MAJOR] Up to eight Daily Mix tiles, with no cap and nothing distinguishing them

- Evidence: SphericalKMeans.chooseK allows k up to 8 (Clustering.kt:14); clusterMixesLocked maps every visible cluster with no take() (TasteEngine.kt:1001-1014); HomeLayout.mixes applies no cap (HomeLayout.kt:184); ShellHome renders them all (ShellHome.kt:187-197,501)
- Why it matters: With four other mix shelves that is up to 12 near-identical "<Artist> Mix" tiles in one horizontal scroll on a phone. Spotify shows about six Made-for-you items and numbers its Daily Mixes. The only size floor is the bridge's 4-item minimum (TurtleHomeShelves.kt:228), so a thin cluster still gets a tile.
- Fix sketch: Cap cluster mixes in clusterMixesLocked (TasteEngine.kt:1003) at about three by cluster weight, add a minimum size like minFallbackShelfSize, and give the rest a "Your top mixes" overflow or a See-all route from ShellHome.kt:485-505.

### [MAJOR] Artist mixes, More like X and Weekly discovery's fallback share the same five radio pages

- Evidence: One RadioCache per refresh capped at shelfUpstreamSeeds = 5 (TasteEngine.kt:916-917, TasteConfig:55); seeds pooled in homeShelves (:515-520) and deduped (HistoryFallback.kt:33); discoverySeedsLocked :955 and artistMixPlanLocked :953 are both topArtistSeedsLocked
- Why it matters: Without clusters the mix seeds and the discovery seeds are the same top artists, so Weekly discovery's fallback is built from the very pages the artist mixes were built from, and the used set at :968 does not extend to it. Three rows meant to feel different become three views of the same five radio pages.
- Fix sketch: Offset the discovery seeds in discoverySeedsLocked (TasteEngine.kt:955) so they start after the mix seeds, raise shelfUpstreamSeeds for the no-cluster path, and extend the artist-mix used set across weeklyDiscoveryLocked and moreLikeLocked.

### [MAJOR] Only one "Because you ..." row ever, and its seed is sticky

- Evidence: homeShelves adds listOfNotNull(pick) for DAILY_MORE_LIKE (TasteEngine.kt:528); moreLikePickLocked returns a single MoreLikePick (:1023-1042) chosen by a deterministic maxWithOrNull over a 30-day window
- Why it matters: Spotify stacks several "Because you listened to" shelves, which is how a listener sees the recommender reasoning about more than one thing. Turtle shows one, and because the pick is the max over a 30-day window it is usually the same track for days, so the row looks frozen next to the "Refreshes daily" line it carries.
- Fix sketch: Make moreLikePickLocked return a short ranked list (TasteEngine.kt:1023-1042), build two or three shelves from it in homeShelves (:513,528), and exclude already-used seeds and artists between them.

### [MAJOR] A mix's cover colour is positional, so it changes when the list changes

- Evidence: MixEntry tint is MIX_TINTS[i % MIX_TINTS.size] with i the index in layout.mixes (ShellHome.kt:196, palette :147); the same tint is carried into the details page (ShellHome.kt:637, ShellMixDetails.kt:286)
- Why it matters: Cover colour is the only thing distinguishing "<Artist> Mix" tiles from each other. Dismissing one mix, or On repeat emptying, re-colours every tile after it, so the mix a listener recognised as the blue one is green tomorrow. Spotify keys Daily Mix colour to the mix identity.
- Fix sketch: Key the tint to shelf identity instead of position: in ShellHome.kt:196 index MIX_TINTS by a stable hash of shelf.key (the engine's shelf id, TasteEngine.kt:913) rather than i.

### [MAJOR] On repeat can list the same song twice from two uploads

- Evidence: onRepeatLocked iterates profile.tracks.values and never uses SongKeys (TasteEngine.kt:1102-1112), unlike compose which goes through QueueAssembler's song-identity dedup (Queue.kt:98-103,130); profile track stats are keyed by TrackKey (TasteProfile.kt:403,358)
- Why it matters: A listener who played both the audio and the video upload of a favourite sees it twice in On repeat, which is the one shelf that is purely their own listening and so the one where a duplicate looks most like a bug. The engine already has the identity machinery for exactly this.
- Fix sketch: Dedup the picks by songs.of(stats.meta) in onRepeatLocked (TasteEngine.kt:1102-1106), keeping the highest recentFinishes, or route On repeat through compose so QueueAssembler's existing usedSongs check applies.

### [MAJOR] Playlist-only rows score zero, so YouTube's recap and community rows never reach default Home

- Evidence: toRowItem maps PlaylistItem to RowItem.Collection(emptyList()) (HomeLayout.kt:300-307, asserted HomeLayoutTest:201); RowScorer skips items with no artists so scoredItems stays 0 (HomeRows.kt:112-117) and fails minScoredItems = 3 (:173); the escape needs isPersonal (:176-179)
- Why it matters: recap_playlists (FEmusic_listening_review, HomeShelfBuilder.kt:290) is the nearest thing to Spotify's Rewind and Wrapped shelves and is marked isPersonal = false, so it can never appear in Matched, the default mode. The maxPersonalPlaylistRows mechanism exists but almost nothing upstream sets the flag.
- Fix sketch: Score playlists by proxy: resolve a few track artists per playlist in the bridge before calling scoreRow, or let RowScorer treat a known browseId allowlist (FEmusic_listening_review, community playlists) as personal in HomeRows.kt:176-179, so one taste-matched playlist row can take a slot.

### [MAJOR] Signed out with artist picks skipped and no library, "Made for you" disappears with no explanation

- Evidence: Mixes need clusters or top-artist seeds, both empty without history, saves or picks (TasteEngine.kt:811-826,936-953); radioCacheLocked disables upstream when profile.tracks is empty (:917); skip() sets PREF_DISMISSED for good (ArtistPicks.kt:188-193); card needs picks.offer (ShellHome.kt:474)
- Why it matters: The listener taps Skip on the artist picks card once and the whole Made for you section vanishes from Home with nothing saying why or how to get it back. Settings can reopen the picks but nothing on Home points there, so the app's headline feature looks broken rather than unconfigured.
- Fix sketch: Keep a persistent empty state in the Made for you slot (ShellHome.kt:468-483) that says mixes appear after some listening and offers to open the picks again, and let coldStart() (TasteEngine.kt:482-485) drive the copy rather than picks.offer alone.

### [MINOR] Weekly discovery is laid out for browsing but plays as a queue

- Evidence: compose(vectorRanked, 0.0, 2, now) takes the default artistSpacing = 1 (TasteEngine.kt:1083,1088 against the signature at :903 and the note at :894-896); cluster mixes pass config.queueRules.artistSpacing = 3 (:1013); the tile's Play starts the whole list as a queue (ShellHome.kt:639-646)
- Why it matters: Discovery songs by one artist can land two apart and play almost back to back, which is the pattern the mixes deliberately avoid. The cap of 2 per artist limits the damage, but a 24-song queue of unfamiliar music is exactly where clustering by artist hurts most.
- Fix sketch: Pass artistSpacing = config.queueRules.artistSpacing in weeklyDiscoveryLocked (TasteEngine.kt:1083,1088), and for consistency in freshForYouLocked (:1126) and rediscoverLocked (:1133), since all four play as queues.

### [MINOR] Kept shelves ignore mutes made after they were built

- Evidence: keptLocked filters only lessLikeThisTracks and mutedArtists (TasteEngine.kt:575-577); it never applies controls.isInMutedCluster (UserControls.kt:62-63) or the dislikedArtistMarks >= 2 rule used elsewhere (HomeRows.kt:214)
- Why it matters: An artist marked "less like this" twice, or a muted taste cluster, keeps appearing in every kept mix until the period turns over: up to a day, or a week for Weekly discovery. The listener's correction appears to have been ignored.
- Fix sketch: Extend the item filter in keptLocked (TasteEngine.kt:575-577) to drop items in a muted cluster and items whose artist has reached config.homeRows.dislikedArtistMarks, mirroring RowScorer.isRuledOut (HomeRows.kt:209-214).

### [MINOR] Six of every thirty built songs are never shown or played

- Evidence: shelfSize is 30 (TasteConfig:37) but toHomeShelf truncates to MAX_ITEMS = 24 (TurtleHomeShelves.kt:236,262); the details page and Play both use the truncated list (ShellHome.kt:189,642; ShellMixDetails.kt:128-134)
- Why it matters: Each refresh does 25% more retrieval, ranking and layout work than the listener can ever see, and the songs dropped are the ones the assembler placed last under the spacing rules, so the discarded tail is not arbitrary. On a phone this is wasted battery every two hours.
- Fix sketch: Set TasteConfig.shelfSize to TurtleHomeShelves.MAX_ITEMS, or pass the UI cap down when constructing TasteConfig in TurtleModule.kt:128-134, so the engine composes exactly what Home can show.

### [MINOR] No way for a listener to refresh their mixes

- Evidence: refreshShelves is deliberately not wired to pull-to-refresh (TasteEngine.kt:559-564) and its only callers are a history or taste change (TurtleRuntime.kt:715) and saving or clearing artist picks (ArtistPicks.kt:180,205)
- Why it matters: Combined with the absence of any rotation mechanism, a listener who dislikes today's mixes has no action at all: they wait until local midnight, or go to Settings and reset their taste. Spotify also keeps the day's mix, but its mixes visibly differ day to day, so the lack of a refresh is not felt.
- Fix sketch: Either add rotation (see the rotation finding) or expose a bounded "build new mixes" action in the Made for you header (ShellHome.kt:488) calling TurtleBridge into taste.refreshShelves plus home.refresh, rate-limited to a few per day.

### [MINOR] Fresh for you stamps the first-seen time as the release date

- Evidence: YouTubeFreshReleases returns tracks with releasedAtMs overwritten by firstSeenMs (YouTubeFreshReleases.kt:130); FreshReleaseGenerator then tests that field against the window (Generators.kt:230) and Ranker weights FRESHNESS from it (Ranker.kt:61-62)
- Why it matters: firstSightFresh (YouTubeFreshReleases.kt:336-342) keeps out anything from an earlier year, but a release from January first seen in December is stamped December, so "New releases from your artists" (strings_home.xml:33) can present an eleven-month-old single as new, and the freshness lift is wrong. The kept shelf persists the false date (KeptShelves.kt:115).
- Fix sketch: Add a firstSeenAtMs field to TrackMeta (turtle/engines/core) and keep releasedAtMs null when YouTube gives only a year; have FreshReleaseGenerator (Generators.kt:230) and the FRESHNESS feature read first-seen explicitly, as the roadmap's catalog-identity item asks.

### [MINOR] Home section titles are not accessibility headings

- Evidence: HomeSectionTitle renders a plain Text on desktop (ShellHome.kt:655-660) and SectionHeader on mobile, which is also a plain Text (ShellComponents.kt:173-180); neither applies semantics { heading() }
- Why it matters: "Made for you", "Jump back in" and every row title are invisible to heading navigation, so a Narrator or TalkBack user has to walk every tile to move between sections of a long Home page. The project's own roadmap lists making section titles semantic headings as an accessibility requirement.
- Fix sketch: Add Modifier.semantics { heading() } to the title Text in ShellHome.kt:657 and ShellComponents.kt:175, and to the mix details header title (ShellMixDetails.kt:270).

### [MINOR] A mix card's artists and its details page's byline are computed differently and can disagree

- Evidence: The card subtitle takes the first 3 distinct artist names across the row's songs (ShellHome.kt:190-195); the page prefers MixAbout.topArtists, which leads with the mix's reason artists then orders by how often each appears (MixAbout.kt:95-111, ShellMixDetails.kt:95-97)
- Why it matters: The tile can say "A, B, C and more" while the page it opens says "B, D, A", so the subtitle a listener chose the mix by does not match what the mix says about itself. It makes an otherwise careful explanation look unreliable.
- Fix sketch: Have HomeLists.of read TurtleBridge.mixAbout(shelf.key)?.topArtists for the subtitle and fall back to the song artists only when it is empty (ShellHome.kt:187-197), reusing the same list the page shows.

### [MINOR] Two clusters led by the same artist get the same title and share one dismissal key

- Evidence: Cluster mix titles use topArtists.first() with "Daily Mix N" only as a name-lookup fallback (TurtleHomeShelves.kt:243-247); Reason.DailyMix.key is "mix:<first top artist>" (Candidates.kt:229), while shelf ids differ (TasteEngine.kt:1013)
- Why it matters: Two tiles can read "<Artist> Mix" with different contents, and dismissing one hides both, now and for every future period. The numbered "Daily Mix %1$d" string already exists (strings.xml:5) but is only reached when the artist name cannot be resolved.
- Fix sketch: Disambiguate in TurtleHomeShelves.title (:242-247): fall back to turtle_shelf_daily_mix when another shown shelf has the same lead artist, and include the cluster id in Reason.DailyMix.key (Candidates.kt:229) so dismissal is per mix.

## Strengths

- **Mixes state the real share of songs the listener already knows, counted over what is actually listed** — ShellMixDetails.kt:154-167 knownPercent and over(shown); aboutWhat refuses to claim anything without counts (:174-208); strings_home.xml:19-35; MixAbout.knownSongs MixAbout.kt:45-49,127 | beats Spotify: Spotify never says how familiar a Daily Mix is. Turtle states it per mix, recounts it against the profile as it is now rather than the share the engine aimed for, and says nothing when it cannot count.
- **Every mix says how often it is rebuilt, in the listener's own time zone** — aboutRefresh ShellMixDetails.kt:211-219; cadenceOf MixAbout.kt:57-64; periodOf TasteEngine.kt:566-569; localDay = deviceDay TurtleModule.kt:71-73,131; strings_home.xml:36-38 | beats Spotify: Spotify leaves refresh cadence to folklore. Turtle prints "Refreshes daily", "Refreshes on Mondays" or "Updates as you listen" per mix, and the periods really do turn over at the listener's midnight, not UTC.
- **Every recommended song carries a typed reason and a parseable origin, written to its play event** — Reason hierarchy Candidates.kt:166-256; SurfaceTag format and parse :107-154; PlayPlace :35-55; shelf item origin TurtleHomeShelves.kt:206-214; Served (ranked, direct, provider, timeout, failed) Candidates.kt:61-81 | beats Spotify: Reasons are data, not marketing copy: the UI localizes a typed reason, and the stored tag even records when the provider's order was kept because ranking timed out or failed, so the app can be honest about who ordered a list.
- **Dismissing a mix is reversible from a page that lists everything hidden** — NotInterested.Mix TurtleRuntime.kt:511-521; hidden() UserControls.kt:105-108; undo() :114-129; stale records pruned TurtleRuntime.kt:625-631 | beats Spotify: Spotify's negative signals are write-only; a listener cannot see or undo them. Turtle lists hidden songs, muted artists and dismissed mixes together with a Show again that also takes back the artist's mark count.
- **All retrieval, clustering and ranking happen on device, and learning stops when history is paused** — EmbeddingStore bound to the on-device CoListeningStore TurtleModule.kt:121-124; graph fed only by radio pages and local plays CoListening.kt:46-70; CoListeningSync.kt:33-41 refuses to learn while history is paused; profile built from the local log TasteEngine.kt:787-800 | beats Spotify: The co-listening signal Spotify builds from everyone's playlists on its servers is derived here from YouTube's own radio pages on the device. No listening profile leaves the phone, and pausing history really does stop the graph learning.
- **The discovery dial bounds the share of unfamiliar songs instead of being a hidden knob** — DiscoveryDial presets and clamp UserControls.kt:10-29; sampleShare clamps the Thompson draw Exploration.kt:115-116; slots spread by error diffusion so discoveries never clump :123-134; persisted TurtleRuntime.kt:650-655 | beats Spotify: The policy adapts on its own but can never leave the range the listener set, and the first slot stays familiar below a 50% share, so a queue never opens on a wall of unknown songs.
- **Saving a mix is one action, not a like on every song** — ShellMixDetails.kt:244-254 expectBulkPlaylistAdd before handing the songs to the playlist picker; PlaylistAddWatcher wired TurtleModule.kt:140; ADDED_TO_PLAYLIST handled separately from likes TasteEngine.kt:247-265 | beats Spotify: Keeping bulk saves out of the taste signal means a listener can archive a mix without distorting what the recommender thinks they love, which is a failure mode of save-driven systems.
- **Layout rules are never relaxed: a shelf gets shorter rather than repetitive** — QueueAssembler breaks when nothing fits instead of loosening (Queue.kt:69-73,144-148); artist spacing, per-artist cap, album-per-hour window and song-identity dedup all hard (:126-142); short fallback shelves are dropped TasteEngine.kt:993,1089 | beats Spotify: Uploads of the same song count as one song everywhere (SongKeys), so a mix never shows the audio and the video of one track as two picks, a common failure of YouTube-backed players.
- **A failing or slow source cannot empty a shelf** — CandidateCollector isolates each generator with a 2s timeout and swallows non-cancellation failures (Generators.kt:336-344); RadioCache prefetches concurrently, per-seed bounded and failure-tolerant (HistoryFallback.kt:36-50); network calls happen outside the engine lock (TasteEngine.kt:522-523) | beats Spotify: Offline, Home still shows the day's kept shelves from disk before any network call (TurtleHomeShelves.kt:83-93,124-140), because the shelves and their songs are persisted rather than refetched.

The same history produces the same clusters, so a listener or auditor can reproduce what the app recommended.
