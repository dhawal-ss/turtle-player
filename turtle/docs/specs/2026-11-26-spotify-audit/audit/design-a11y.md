## Weaknesses

### [BLOCKER] Android system bars and window background follow upstream's light theme while Shell stays dark

- Evidence: androidApp/.../ui/theme/Theme.kt:66-72 sets isAppearanceLightStatusBars and LightNavigationBars from upstream's darkTheme; :130-133 paints the window background from upstream's colorScheme.surface. Settings.kt:323 defaults themeMode to AUTO.
- Why it matters: On a phone in light system theme the status-bar clock and gesture pill are drawn dark over Shell's #0B0B0C ground and tab bar, so they are effectively invisible, and the window background flashes light. The theme-mode setting that would force dark is hidden (ShellSettingsTurtle.kt:197, :716), so the listener has no in-app fix.
- Fix sketch: Either expose a Shell theme-mode control, or force the bar appearance from Shell: add a TurtleUiSlots flag read in androidApp/src/main/kotlin/com/metrolist/android/ui/theme/Theme.kt at lines 66-72 and 130-133 so bars and window background follow ShellThemes.active.ground while a Shell palette is installed. Record the hook in turtle/HOOKS.md.

### [MAJOR] Dynamic palette picks onAccent by a luminance cutoff, failing AA across half the hue wheel

- Evidence: ShellPalettes.kt:113-114 (onAccent = dark if luminance > 0.45f else light); :144 lightens only until the accent reads on surface3. Swept at HEAD: reds, magentas, violets and blues give 1.97-3.23:1; the real crossover is ~0.18 luminance.
- Why it matters: onAccent is the glyph and label colour on every accent-filled control: selected ShellChip (ShellComponents.kt:159-164), selected RailItem (ShellNavigation.kt:322-331), HoverPlayButton (:218, :226), the snackbar Undo button (:468-470). With a blue or red cover the selected tab label drops below 3.3:1. Named in the HEAD roadmap, unfixed.
- Fix sketch: In ShellPalettes.kt vivid() and FollowCover: pick whichever of the dark and light inks gives the higher contrast() against the candidate accent, and keep raising lightness until that ink reaches READABLE_CONTRAST, not just until the accent reads on surface3. contrast() (:152) and readableTint() (:162) already exist. Add a hue-sweep test.

### [MAJOR] White labels on fixed card and tile tints fail AA, badly once artwork is composited

- Evidence: MixCard: white over the tint, art at 0.55 alpha (ShellComponents.kt:299-310); tints at ShellHome.kt:147. Measured amber 1.95, green 3.34, pink 4.21; over a light cover all seven fall to 1.5-2.2:1. ShellExplore.kt:85 tints: 8 of 11 fail on hover. ShellSearch.kt:159: 3 of 8 fail.
- Why it matters: MixCard is Home's headline surface, the Daily Mix equivalent, and the mix name is often unreadable against a bright cover because nothing scrims the title. Explore and Search category tiles are the main browse entry points. The HEAD roadmap names the Search and Explore ratios but not the MixCard composite or the hover blend.
- Fix sketch: Run every tint through readableTint(bg, listOf(Color.White)) from ShellPalettes.kt:162, evaluated on the hover and selected composites too. For MixCard (ShellComponents.kt:299-310) add a bottom scrim behind the title rather than relying on the 0.55-alpha art. Replace the fixed lists at ShellHome.kt:147, ShellExplore.kt:85 and ShellSearch.kt:159.

### [MAJOR] ShellChip is a 32dp target and announces itself as a tab even when it is a Retry button

- Evidence: ShellComponents.kt:157-165: height(32.dp), role = Role.Tab, selected always passed. Used as a button at ShellArtist.kt:1029, :1040, ShellSearch.kt:341, :360, :495, ShellPodcastsPage.kt:176, :212, ShellAudiobooks.kt:190, ShellSpoken.kt:296-308.
- Why it matters: Home's filter chips, Search's filters and vibes, the library filters and every Retry action are 32dp tall, under the 48dp Android and WCAG 2.5.5 target, and a screen reader hears 'Retry, tab, not selected'. ShellExplore.kt:202-210 already wraps a 32dp pill in a 48dp target and says why, so the fix was never pushed into the component.
- Fix sketch: Give ShellChip a role parameter (Role.Tab only for real filters), stop passing selected for buttons, and wrap it as ShellExplore.kt:203-210 does, or add minimumInteractiveComponentSize() inside shellPressable (ShellComponents.kt:101) as ShellLibrary.kt:644 already does. Same for the 36-40dp buttons at ShellHome.kt:394, ShellComponents.kt:474, ShellArtist.kt:631, :763.

### [MAJOR] Shell's entire UI is English-only while upstream ships 58 locales

- Evidence: 522 distinct keys in 15 files under turtle/ui/src/commonMain/composeResources/values/ and no values-* directory under turtle/. No shell_ key exists in shared/src/commonMain/composeResources/values-*/strings.xml.
- Why it matters: Shell replaces Home, Search, Library, the player, the artist, album and playlist pages, Settings, menus and dialogs, so a listener in Hindi or Arabic now sees an English app where Metrolist was translated. For ar, fa, iw and ur the result is English text in an RTL layout. A regression against upstream, not just a gap.
- Fix sketch: Decide where Shell's catalogue lives: its own Weblate component under turtle/ui/src/commonMain/composeResources, or merged into shared/src/commonMain/composeResources/values/strings.xml behind the shell_ prefix so Weblate picks it up. Then seed values-* for the top locales. Root CLAUDE.md forbids platform-local catalogs, so this needs an owner decision first.

### [MAJOR] An element's accessible label overrides the meta and subtitle a sighted user reads

- Evidence: shellPressable puts contentDescription on the clickable node (ShellComponents.kt:119-124). LibraryRowView labels only the title while drawing row.meta (ShellDesktop.kt:515, :531). UpNextCard labels only the title while drawing artists (:725, :732). MixCard the same (ShellComponents.kt:296, :318).
- Why it matters: Screen-reader users hear 'Discover Weekly' where a sighted user reads the artists inside it, and 'Album' instead of 'Album, Radiohead'. The who-is-in-it line is what makes a mix choosable. Palette swatches have the same shape of bug: ShellAppearance.kt:83-85 passes a label but no selected, so the active palette is never announced.
- Fix sketch: Compose the full spoken string where a row has secondary text: label = "$title, $meta" at ShellDesktop.kt:515 and :725, label = "$title, $subtitle" at ShellComponents.kt:296. Add selected = selected at ShellAppearance.kt:85. Add stateDescription for the playing row, signalled today only by accent colour plus the equalizer (ShellDesktop.kt:530-535).

### [MAJOR] No reduced-motion support anywhere in Shell; five infinite animations always run

- Evidence: ShellTheme.kt:74-88 has no gate. Infinite loops: Equalizer at ShellComponents.kt:185-191, Home pulse at ShellHome.kt:821-822, Search at ShellSearch.kt:515-516, Library at ShellLibrary.kt:985, Lyrics at ShellLyrics.kt:537-547.
- Why it matters: The equalizer bounces on every playing row in the desktop sidebar, on shortcut tiles and in the library list at once, and the loading pulses run on four screens. There is no way to stop any of it and Shell's settings offer no motion control. Upstream's rememberReducedMotion is internal to :shared and returns false on Android and desktop, so there is nothing to reuse.
- Fix sketch: Add a Shell-owned reducedMotion flag: a preference key plus a composition local beside ShellMotion in ShellTheme.kt, defaulting from the coroutine MotionDurationScale. Gate the five infinite transitions and have ShellMotion.emphasized/spring/exit return snap() when it is on. Expose the toggle in Turtle's Theme page (ShellSettingsTurtle.kt:294-308).

### [MAJOR] Overlays do not contain keyboard focus; the narrow desktop panel does not even hide the page

- Evidence: hiddenUnderBrowse uses clearAndSetSemantics {} (ShellBrowse.kt:123), which removes semantics but not focusability; applied at ShellNavigation.kt:148, :157, :302 and ShellDesktop.kt:111. CompactPanelOverlay (ShellDesktop.kt:260-279) adds neither, and its scrim is a bare pointerInput Box (:150-153).
- Why it matters: With Browse or Explore open, Tab walks into the invisible page underneath and the focus ring is drawn on a hidden control (WCAG 2.4.3, 2.4.7). In a narrow desktop window the page under the queue panel stays fully readable by Narrator and fully focusable. The Dialog-based surfaces (ShellDialogs.kt:41, ShellAppearance.kt:59) behave correctly, so the overlays are the odd ones out.
- Fix sketch: Extend Modifier.hiddenUnderBrowse (ShellBrowse.kt:123) to add focusProperties { canFocus = false } on a focusGroup, and apply it to the page under CompactPanelOverlay in ShellDesktop.kt:110-120. Give the scrim at :150-153 a dismiss role and contentDescription, and move initial focus into the overlay when it opens.

### [MAJOR] Directional glyphs do not mirror in RTL even though RTL is enabled and shipped

- Evidence: Glyphs.kt:21-31 calls ImageVector.Builder without autoMirror. ArrowRight/ArrowLeft at :59-60, ChevronRight at :73, SkipBack/SkipForward at :75-76. AndroidManifest.xml:75 sets supportsRtl="true"; ar, fa, iw and ur catalogs ship.
- Why it matters: In Arabic, Hebrew, Persian and Urdu the sidebar 'Open library' arrow (ShellDesktop.kt:469), the 'back to Now Playing' arrow (:614) and every list chevron point away from where they navigate, inverting the meaning of the control. Layout is otherwise RTL-correct, so the icons are the only thing fighting the reading direction.
- Fix sketch: Add an autoMirror parameter to Glyphs.vector (Glyphs.kt:21-31) and set it true for ArrowRight, ArrowLeft, ChevronRight, SkipBack, SkipForward, Share and Download. Leave the transport glyphs (Play, Pause, Next, Previous) unmirrored per Material's media-control guidance.

### [MAJOR] Focus ring is accent-on-accent, so focus is invisible on selected and accent-filled controls

- Evidence: shellPressable draws border(2.dp, Shell.colors.accent, focusShape) (ShellComponents.kt:117). Accent-filled: selected ShellChip (:159-162), selected RailItem (ShellNavigation.kt:322-325), HoverPlayButton (:223-224), PlayPauseButton on accent (:326, from ShellHome.kt:617-619), snackbar Undo (:469).
- Why it matters: A keyboard user tabbing across Home's filter chips or the tablet rail gets no visible focus state on the selected item (WCAG 2.4.7). The ring is also drawn after an earlier clip(), so it is cut where the shapes differ: ShellChip clips to a circle but gets the default 8dp rect (:162), RailItem clips to 18dp (ShellNavigation.kt:325), NavRow and LibraryRowView to 6dp (ShellDesktop.kt:350, :514).
- Fix sketch: In ShellComponents.kt:117 draw the ring in Shell.colors.ink or ground with a small outset instead of accent, or choose the ring colour by contrast against the element's own background. Pass the matching focusShape at ShellComponents.kt:162, ShellNavigation.kt:325, ShellDesktop.kt:350 and :514, as HoverPlayButton and the snackbar already do.

### [MAJOR] Shell's typography never reaches the app on Android

- Evidence: TurtleUiSlots.typography is consumed only at shared/.../ui/theme/CommonTheme.kt:108. Android uses androidApp/.../ui/theme/Theme.kt:57, which sets typography = getAppTypography() at :138. ShellTheme.kt:139 calls MaterialTheme without a typography argument, so it inherits the outer one.
- Why it matters: On Android, Space Grotesk and Inter apply only where Shell sets a style explicitly; every upstream-drawn Text inside Shell's takeover (settings sub-pages, equalizer, history, statistics, Material components in menus) uses upstream's type scale, so headings and body text do not match. turtle/HOOKS.md:55 claims hook #32 covers 'the whole app'.
- Fix sketch: Pass typography = shellMaterialTypography() in ShellTheme (ShellTheme.kt:136-141), which fixes Android without a new hook and leaves desktop unchanged. The alternative is a one-line #32 hook in androidApp/src/main/kotlin/com/metrolist/android/ui/theme/Theme.kt:138, recorded in turtle/HOOKS.md.

### [MAJOR] Section titles are not headings outside Settings

- Evidence: SectionHeader (ShellComponents.kt:173-180) sets no semantics; callers at ShellExplore.kt:178, :222, :258, :292, ShellHome.kt:662, ShellSpoken.kt:383. Only ShellSettings.kt:190, :338, :416 use heading().
- Why it matters: Heading navigation is the fastest way down a long feed for TalkBack and Narrator. On Home, Explore and Spoken word a screen-reader user must step through every card of 'Made for you' to reach the next shelf. Named in the HEAD roadmap ('Make section titles semantic headings'), unfixed.
- Fix sketch: Add Modifier.semantics { heading() } to the title Text in SectionHeader (ShellComponents.kt:175); all six callers then inherit it. Do the same for the one-off shelf titles in ShellHome.kt and ShellLibrary.kt.

### [MAJOR] Fixed row and tile heights clip their text at large font scales

- Evidence: ShortcutTile 56dp, maxLines 2 (ShellComponents.kt:251, :255); LibraryRowView 64dp, two lines (ShellDesktop.kt:514, :530); mini player 56dp, two lines (ShellPlayer.kt:371, :406); NavRow 44dp (ShellDesktop.kt:350); search field 34dp (:745); tab bar 68dp (ShellNavigation.kt:129).
- Why it matters: At the large font sizes a low-vision user relies on, titles and subtitles are cut mid-line instead of the row growing; ShortcutTile's two 16sp lines already need 64dp at 2x, and AppearanceDensity.kt:44 scales small phones further still. The codebase knows the pattern: heightIn at ShellComponents.kt:458, a scrolling chip row at ShellDesktop.kt:473-474, a FlowRow at :710-713.
- Fix sketch: Replace height() with heightIn(min = ) on those rows and tiles (ShellComponents.kt:251, ShellDesktop.kt:350, :514, :745, ShellPlayer.kt:371, ShellNavigation.kt:129) and let content set the height. Add a 2x font-scale variant to the existing shot tests under turtle/ui/src/desktopTest/kotlin/app/turtleplayer/ui/shot/ so clipping shows up.

### [MAJOR] Dark-only with no escape: no light palette, no contrast control, no way back to upstream's theme

- Evidence: ShellTheme.kt:169 always builds a darkColorScheme; all eleven palettes are dark (ShellPalettes.kt:62-74); TurtleUiSlots.kt:38-42 states Shell is dark only. Shell hides themeMode (ShellSettingsTurtle.kt:197, :716) and never reads the Android contrast level (androidApp/.../ui/theme/Theme.kt:80).
- Why it matters: Listeners who need a light or high-contrast interface (low vision, astigmatism, bright sunlight) have no option at all, and Android's own 'Increase contrast' setting has no effect inside Turtle. Metrolist offered light, dark, auto, pure black and a system contrast level; Shell removed all five.
- Fix sketch: Either add at least one light and one high-contrast palette to ShellPalettes.all (ShellPalettes.kt:62) and switch shellMaterialScheme to lightColorScheme for a light palette (ShellTheme.kt:167-207), or expose themeMode again in Turtle's Theme page and have ShellTheme follow it. Reading the Android contrast level into an ink and accent boost is a smaller first step.

### [MINOR] The seek and volume bars are not focusable and show no focus state

- Evidence: ShellSeekBar (ShellPlayer.kt:249-305) attaches only pointerInput handlers (:278-289) and animates the knob on hover (:267-274); there is no focusable(), onKeyEvent or focused branch. Used at :578 and :816.
- Why it matters: Desktop keyboard users can still seek with the global Left/Right shortcuts (DesktopShortcuts.kt:477-485) and change volume with Up/Down (:456-459), so the loss is limited. But on Android with a hardware keyboard, and for anyone tabbing through the player, the bar cannot be reached or operated and nothing shows it has focus; only the setProgress path works (:326).
- Fix sketch: Add Modifier.focusable(interactionSource) and an onKeyEvent handler for Left/Right (PageUp/PageDown for larger steps) to the outer Box at ShellPlayer.kt:276, and draw the knob when focused as well as hovered (:267-274).

### [MINOR] Disabled menu tiles carry no semantics at all

- Evidence: ShellMenus.kt:258-260: shellPressable is applied only when action.enabled, so a disabled PrimaryTile has no role, no onClick and no disabled() semantics; its tint drops to ink2 at 0.5 alpha (:254).
- Why it matters: A screen reader reads a disabled 'Play next' or 'Download' tile as plain text, with no clue that it is a control or why it cannot be used, while sighted users see a greyed button. The same tile is the main action surface in every media menu.
- Fix sketch: In ShellMenus.kt:258-260 always apply a clickable or semantics modifier and add Modifier.semantics { disabled() } plus the role when action.enabled is false, rather than omitting shellPressable.

### [MINOR] ShortcutTile's play button appears only on hover, with no focus or screen-reader route

- Evidence: ShellComponents.kt:259 passes HoverPlayButton(hovered, ...) with no focus term, and the tile adds no custom action (:250-251). MixCard in the same file does it correctly: focus at :290 and :294, visibility at :313, Play as a CustomAccessibilityAction at :295.
- Why it matters: On the desktop Home shortcut grid, Play is unreachable by keyboard and invisible to a screen reader; on touch it never appears at all, since touch screens do not hover, so the only way to play a shortcut is to open it first.
- Fix sketch: Copy MixCard's approach into ShortcutTile (ShellComponents.kt:236-261): track onFocusChanged, pass hovered || focusWithin || !ShellPlatform.desktop to HoverPlayButton, and add customActions = listOf(CustomAccessibilityAction(playLabel) { onPlay(); true }) when onPlay is non-null.

### [MINOR] No 'See all' anywhere; the SectionHeader action API has zero callers

## Open questions

- Is dark-only deliberate? themeMode is hidden (ShellSettingsTurtle.kt:197, :716) yet still drives Android's status bars and window background (androidApp/.../ui/theme/Theme.kt:66-72, :132). Expose a Shell control, or force the bars from Shell?
- Where should Shell's 522 strings be localized? Root CLAUDE.md forbids platform-local catalogs and turtle/AGENTS.md says the UI localizes through upstream's resources, yet turtle/ui has its own catalog with no values-*.
- Should the palette contrast invariant become a test? ShellPalettes.kt:59-61 asserts it only in a comment; a sweep over ShellPalettes.all plus vivid() across the hue wheel would be cheap and pure Kotlin.
- Should Shell own a reduced-motion preference? Upstream's rememberReducedMotion is internal to :shared and returns false on Android and desktop, so Shell cannot reuse it and has no toggle of its own.
- Does the owner want a keyboard-shortcut help surface on Windows? DesktopMenuBar, the only place the shortcuts are written down, is gated to macOS at desktopApp/.../Main.kt:1379, and Windows is in the launch scope.
- Should the index-on-surface3 exclusion be enforced rather than assumed? It is the only measured contrast failure in the static palettes and rests on the claim that IndexLabel is never drawn on a hovered surface3 row.
- Is a light or high-contrast palette in scope for v1? Android's 'Increase contrast' currently has no effect in Turtle (androidApp/.../ui/theme/Theme.kt:80 versus ShellTheme.kt:136-141).
