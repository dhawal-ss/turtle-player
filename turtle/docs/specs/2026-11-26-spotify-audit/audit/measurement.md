## Weaknesses

### [BLOCKER] Songs YouTube types PODCAST_EPISODE are never recorded, and they also break run splitting

- Evidence: turtle/bridge/src/commonMain/kotlin/app/turtleplayer/bridge/PlayerObserver.kt:274 drops them before any signal; TurtleSpoken.kt:304-305 (isPodcast alone); shared/.../model/VideoType.kt:18, :27 and Models.kt:482-485
- Why it matters: These plays vanish from history, taste and every measurement, and the untagged share cannot reveal the loss. Mid-run the previous song closes STOPPED (PlayerObserver.kt:276), the next music song gets previous==null so its start is UNKNOWN (:284), and UNKNOWN is a deliberate restart (EarlyRuns.kt:47), so the run splits.
- Fix sketch: Classify spoken word on more than the video type: require a podcast browse or show id, an episode page type, or a Turtle-opened chapter. Touch shared/src/commonMain/kotlin/com/metrolist/shared/model/VideoType.kt, Models.kt:482-485 and turtle/bridge/.../TurtleSpoken.kt:304-305. Until then record the play and mark it spoken rather than discarding it, so the loss is countable.

### [MAJOR] Playback errors are never recorded locally, so a failed start reads as an instant skip

- Evidence: Only COMPLETED and STOPPED are ever sent (PlayerObserver.kt:157, :276); inferEnd never yields ERROR (ListeningSessionTracker.kt:255-266). FirstFiveSkips' own KDoc says so (ListenerOutcomes.kt:94-95), and INTERRUPTED can only match STOPPED (:99).
- Why it matters: instantSkips is presented as the diagnostic for failed starts (ListenerOutcomes.kt:33-39) but cannot separate them from real skips, so every per-surface early-skip rate is inflated by an unknown amount. The same false skip reaches TasteProfile as the strongest negative signal, so a surface serving unplayable uploads is punished twice.
- Fix sketch: Upstream already records playback failures locally with a videoId (shared/.../youtube/playback/PlaybackEventLog.kt:58-60, PlaybackEvent.kt:12-37) and Turtle already consumes that stream (DriveLog.kt:46-54). Route PLAYER and STREAM error events for the current videoId into PlayerObserver as TrackEnded(EndReason.ERROR), and teach ListeningSessionTracker.inferEnd about it.

### [MAJOR] Two early-skip numbers that disagree, printed asymmetrically by the probe

- Evidence: OriginReporter counts an UNKNOWN end under 30 s as a non-skip and ignores joined runs (OriginReport.kt:114-120); FirstFiveSkips puts it in `unknown`, outside the rate, and excludes joined (ListenerOutcomes.kt:120-130). LocalOriginReportProbe.kt:30-36 vs :40-41.
- Why it matters: A reviewer quoting the origin table understates skips; the outcomes table gives a different denominator. The one recorded reading (2026-11-23-discovery-first-review.md, Step 2 baseline: Infinity 16 of 23) came from the looser table, so the plan's headline numbers are not the numbers the gate would use.
- Fix sketch: Drop the early-skip table from OriginReport.kt:56-65 and keep OriginReporter for the plays-by-origin baseline only, so FirstFiveSkips is the single definition. Then print FirstFiveSkips for each of the three history readings in LocalOriginReportProbe.kt:30-41. FirstFiveSkipsTest.kt:204 already asserts where the two agree.

### [MAJOR] StartReason.UNKNOWN counts as a deliberate run restart, so with no live controller every play is slot 1

- Evidence: DELIBERATE_STARTS includes UNKNOWN (EarlyRuns.kt:47), used at :67-68 and :78. The observer returns UNKNOWN whenever no queue was sampled or no controller exists (PlayerObserver.kt:306-316, :324) and after app start (OriginReport.kt:94).
- Why it matters: Screen off, in the car, on Android Auto or after a launch, run structure collapses: each play restarts its run, so slot counts and denominators are meaningless in exactly the sessions that also produce false skips. The metric's name stops describing what it counts.
- Fix sketch: Split the reason: keep TAPPED (and a new confirmed replaced-queue reason) as deliberate restarts and treat UNKNOWN as neither a restart nor a slot. Changes EarlyRuns.kt:46-48 and :63-83, and needs PlayerObserver.classify (:304-332) to distinguish a confirmed queue replacement from "could not tell". Report an unknown-start share per surface beside the rate.

### [MAJOR] Whole surfaces have no PlayPlace, so album, artist, playlist, library, queue and Explore plays are untagged

- Evidence: PlayPlace has nine values, none for those surfaces (Candidates.kt:35-55). attributePlay has two callers: ShellHomeFeedback.kt:64 and ShellSearch.kt:696. ShellExplore.kt:263, :272, :309 and ShellArtist.kt:696, :804 call playback.play directly.
- Why it matters: 70% untagged in the one recorded reading, and the report cannot tell "untagged because the surface has no place" from "untagged because attribution failed". Explore's Popular picks and genre tiles are new at HEAD and can never be measured. Any per-surface comparison is drawn from a minority of plays.
- Fix sketch: Append PlayPlace entries for EXPLORE, ALBUM, ARTIST, PLAYLIST, LIBRARY and QUEUE in turtle/engines/taste/.../Candidates.kt:35-55 (tags are persisted, so append only), then call TurtleBridge.attributePlay from ShellExplore.kt:263 and :309, ShellArtist.kt:696, ShellCollection and ShellBrowse. Keep them out of DiscoveryReturn.PAIR_PLACES so only denominators change.

### [MAJOR] An untagged play before a tagged run marks the whole run joined, so the run is never eligible

- Evidence: EarlyRuns.kt:70-72 sets joined when a run is entered from an untagged play (place == null) or at session start with a ran-on reason; ListenerOutcomes.kt:121 then counts every slot of that run as joined and out of the rate.
- Why it matters: With the untagged surfaces above, this removes a large and non-random share of radio, Infinity and engine runs from the first-five rate: exactly the runs a listener reached by letting an album or playlist run on. The remaining sample is biased toward sessions that began on Home or Search.
- Fix sketch: Keep joined as the honest bucket it is but make it recoverable: once the surfaces above are tagged, a run entered from a tagged album or playlist play is no longer joined. Also render joined as a share beside the rate in FirstFiveReport.render (ListenerOutcomes.kt:63-82), as unknownRate already is, so a reviewer sees how much of the surface was dropped.

### [MAJOR] No listener-outcome evidence exists yet: measurement started at HEAD, no discovery window has closed

- Evidence: PREF_MEASURE_SINCE and LABEL_VERSION both landed in 09ae8be7 (git log -S, 24 Nov 01:17); SaveWatcher.kt:85-88 writes it on first launch. DiscoveryReturn's window is 7 days (ListenerOutcomes.kt:238) and unclosed pairs are pending (:286-288).
- Why it matters: Every listener-outcome number available today is pending or built on hours of data from one device. The only concrete figures (284 plays, 70% untagged, 5-23 plays per surface) are doc-reported from 23 Nov, before the fixes, and the plan itself calls them "not a finding".
- Fix sketch: Treat the first closed 7-day window as a release gate: record in turtle/docs/plans/2026-11-23-status.md the earliest date a DiscoveryReturn number can exist, and add a minimum-sample rule to FirstFiveReport.render and DiscoveryReport.render (ListenerOutcomes.kt:63-82, :170-197) that prints "too few" instead of a percentage below a stated threshold.

### [MAJOR] The replay gate is saturated and partly circular

- Evidence: GOLDEN_RECALL_NEW == GOLDEN_STANDARD_RECALL_NEW == 0.960 (OfflineReplayTest.kt:94, :100), so every new-only holdout fits in K and the metric sits near its ceiling. Histories and track vectors share the same genre centroids (SyntheticWorld.kt:33-57, :100-121).
- Why it matters: A recall of 0.96 with a 2% band leaves almost no room to fall, and the discovery targets are drawn from the same genre structure the engine's vectors encode, so the engine recovers a label it was handed. The gate mostly proves the pipeline runs.
- Fix sketch: Make the world harder before touching thresholds: add listeners with drifting taste, overlapping genres, sparse history and out-of-taste favourites in SyntheticWorld.kt:84-143, and add noise between the vector space and the generating preference. Then re-measure and record new goldens in OfflineReplayTest.kt:90-101 with the reason in the PR.

### [MAJOR] The replay exercises a path no listener experiences: no tags, no queue rules, no shelves, no controls

- Evidence: TasteEngineRecommender builds a bare engine (ReplayEvaluator.kt:23-28) and calls recommendations(), "without queue rules" (TasteEngine.kt:660-668). SyntheticWorld.engine defaults fresh, upstream and blocks to null (SyntheticWorld.kt:145-157); events carry no surfaceDetail (:133-135).
- Why it matters: Artist spacing, dedup, discovery share, Not interested, muted artists and clusters, the discovery dial and every per-source skip rate learned from tags all sit outside the gate. A change that wrecks the queue the listener actually hears passes CI.
- Fix sketch: Add a surface-level Recommender adapter per surface (autoplay queue, daily mixes, weekly discovery, Home rows) under turtle/engines/taste/.../eval/, give SyntheticWorld histories real surfaceDetail tags via SurfaceTag.format, and populate controls and blocks for at least one cohort. Gate the queue adapter at 2% alongside the existing numbers.

### [MAJOR] One seed, 30 listeners, no variance estimate; novelty and coverage printed but never gated

- Evidence: OfflineReplayTest.kt:21-22 fixes SyntheticWorld(seed=42) and users 0..29; assertNoRegression checks only recall, NDCG and standard recall (:81-88). Novelty and coverage reach only println (:47-49, :61-63) through EvalMetrics.toString.
- Why it matters: A 2% band with no confidence interval cannot separate a real regression from seed noise, and a change that collapses novelty or catalog coverage to raise recall passes silently: the classic way a recommender gets worse while its headline metric improves.
- Fix sketch: Evaluate over several seeds (for example five worlds of 30 listeners) in OfflineReplayTest.kt:20-40, assert on the mean with the spread printed, and add floors for novelty and coverage next to the recall assertions at :81-88.

### [MAJOR] The holdout is collapsed to a set of track keys, so play counts, days, likes and saves are discarded

- Evidence: ReplayEvaluator.kt:79-83 maps the holdout window to a Set of trackKey; evaluate() at :73 accepts no TrackFeedback. PlayEvent carries liked, replayed and addedToPlaylist (PlayEvent.kt:78-81) and the evaluator ignores all three.
- Why it matters: A track played twenty times and a track played once score identically, and relevance is binary in the NDCG too (:107). The gate cannot reward recommendations the listener kept, which is precisely what DiscoveryReturn measures on device, so the offline and on-device definitions of a good recommendation disagree.
- Fix sketch: Add a RETAINED holdout target (never heard in training, and in the holdout finished on two or more separate days, liked, or saved), accept an optional per-user List<TrackFeedback> in evaluate(), and report a graded NDCG using play count as gain. Changes ReplayEvaluator.kt:31-36, :73-83, :106-110 and SyntheticWorld.history.

### [MAJOR] No evidence path a non-developer can use, and nothing persists a measurement over time

- Evidence: The reports are developer diagnostics "never shown in the app" (ListenerOutcomes.kt:20, OriginReport.kt:42); the only reader is a desktopTest probe needing Gradle, adb and a DB copy migrated in place (LocalOriginReportProbe.kt:16-19, :26). No turtle/ui file references any eval class.
- Why it matters: A beta tester cannot produce the numbers, the owner cannot produce them away from a desktop machine, and nothing stores yesterday's figure, so no trend or before/after comparison is possible. Every reading is a one-off screenshot of one device.
- Fix sketch: Add a Turtle diagnostics page under the Devtools link Shell already keeps (ShellSettingsTurtle.kt:218-235, :618-621) rendering FirstFiveReport.render() and DiscoveryReport.render() with a copy action. The eval classes already ship in the app's commonMain, so this is UI only. Separately append each build's rendered report to a capped local file.

### [MAJOR] Bridge and Shell tests, including the probe and every attribution test, run only on release tags

- Evidence: .github/workflows/turtle-engines.yml:38-39 runs only `-p turtle/engines`; `:turtle-bridge:desktopTest` appears once in the repository, at .github/workflows/turtle-release.yml:180, whose trigger is `push: tags: ['v*.*.*']` (:7-9).
- Why it matters: Attribution is where measurement is won or lost, and PlayerObserverTest, SaveWatcherTest and TasteHistoryLensTest (all added in HEAD) do not run on a pull request. A change that silently stops tagging plays, or stops writing SAVED rows, merges green.
- Fix sketch: Add a bridge job to .github/workflows/turtle-engines.yml running `./gradlew :turtle-bridge:desktopTest` and `:turtle-ui:desktopTest` on pull_request, reusing the prepare-ci-gradle action the release workflow already uses.

### [MAJOR] Maturity counts imports and ignores a taste reset, so reports say established while the engine is cold

- Evidence: rateAll counts every event, imports included (EarlyRuns.kt:14-31). The engine instead reads through the lens, which drops plays up to ignoreThroughId (HistoryLens.kt:18-19, :34; TurtleRuntime.kt:669-673). The probe hands the reports raw rows (LocalOriginReportProbe.kt:28, :40-41).
- Why it matters: Right after a reset the engine is in cold start (TasteEngine.kt:479-484) and shows unscored rows, while the report files those plays under ESTABLISHED. The cohort split, the one thing that would explain a bad early-skip rate, points at the wrong cohort. An importer is never COLD, so that arm is unmeasurable for them.
- Fix sketch: Give the reporters the engine's lens: pass HistoryLens-read events from LocalOriginReportProbe.kt:40-41, and add an optional reset cutoff to rateAll (EarlyRuns.kt:19) so maturity counts only what the engine counts. Also split maturity on local versus imported finishes, so an importer still has a cold arm.

### [MAJOR] No latency or release-guardrail measurement anywhere

- Evidence: The roadmap names time to first playable result and guardrails (start failures, time-to-audio p95, leakage, exposure concentration) in 2026-11-23-discovery-release-roadmap.md. No timer, counter or percentile exists in turtle/engines or turtle/bridge/commonMain; TurtleLog.kt:12-22 only prints.
- Why it matters: The two gates that would actually stop a bad public beta, how long until music plays and how often it fails, have no number at all. Radio and Infinity already label timeout and failed fallbacks on plays (Candidates.kt:72-75), so timeouts are recorded but never counted as a rate.
- Fix sketch: Count what is already labelled first: add a served-path breakdown (ranked vs timeout vs failed) as a rate in OriginReport.render (OriginReport.kt:43-66). Then stamp the tap in ShellHomeFeedback.play and the first audible frame in PlayerObserver.start (:334-383) and render p50/p95 with an abandonment count in the diagnostics page.

### [MAJOR] Turtle's own log drives no listener-facing view, and the stats screen reads a different history

- Evidence: Upstream's StatsViewModel reads upstream's PlayHistoryRepository (StatsViewModel.kt:49-57); every Turtle measurement reads turtle.db (LocalOriginReportProbe.kt:26, SqlitePlayEventStore.kt:507-516). Turtle's log also excludes spoken word and paused-history plays.
- Why it matters: The listener sees one set of totals while the engine learns from another, so someone checking "did it learn what I played?" gets an answer from the wrong table. Turtle's richer log (origin, serving path, skip reasons, maturity) powers nothing the listener can see, which is its most Spotify-beating asset.
- Fix sketch: Build a Turtle listening recap on turtle.db using the existing renderers plus TasteProfile: top artists and songs, how much came from each surface, the discovery share actually delivered, and what was saved. New screen under turtle/ui/src/commonMain/.../screens/ reading PlayEventStore and turtle/engines/taste/.../eval/. State plainly which plays are excluded.

### [MINOR] The search golden gate's 2% band on a saturated metric lets one query break silently

- Evidence: GOLDEN_TOP1 and GOLDEN_TOP3 are both 1.0 (GoldenQuerySetTest.kt:122-125) with assertions at `>= golden * 0.98` (:118-119) over 96 queries (96 `Category.` rows in GoldenQueries.kt).
- Why it matters: 95/96 = 0.9896, which clears the 0.98 floor, so one query may start failing with a green build; only a second regression trips it. On a perfect baseline the right rule is exact equality.
- Fix sketch: When the golden value is 1.0, assert equality (allow zero misses) in GoldenQuerySetTest.kt:118-119, and keep the relative band only for metrics below ceiling. Growing the set toward the planned 500 queries also restores headroom.

### [MINOR] PlayEvent.addedToPlaylist is never true for a local play

- Evidence: ListeningSessionTracker.kt:237 sets it from Feedback(ADDED_TO_PLAYLIST), but nothing sends that kind: the only senders are LIKED, UNLIKED and LESS_LIKE_THIS (TurtleRuntime.kt:463, :469, :484). PlaylistAddWatcher writes only a TrackFeedback row (PlaylistAdds.kt:188-191).
- Why it matters: DiscoveryReturn's `plays.any { it.liked || it.addedToPlaylist }` (ListenerOutcomes.kt:291) carries a term that can never fire, reading as coverage the metric does not have. It also means a playlist add never counts as a discovery success (Exploration.kt:97) or an engagement bonus (SessionModel.kt:97).
- Fix sketch: Have PlaylistAddWatcher.recordDue (PlaylistAdds.kt:174-191) also call observer.feedback(FeedbackKind.ADDED_TO_PLAYLIST) when the added song is the one playing, as watchLikes does for likes (TurtleRuntime.kt:455-470). Otherwise drop the dead term from ListenerOutcomes.kt:291 and say the feedback table is the only source.

### [MINOR] Two different pause policies for saves, mixed into one saved rate

- Evidence: PlaylistAddWatcher records adds while history is paused, by design (PlaylistAdds.kt:114-118). SaveWatcher drops likes and library adds while paused and permanently forgets an add first seen while paused (SaveWatcher.kt:31-35, :91, :99, :111-113).
- Why it matters: DiscoveryReturn ORs both sources (ListenerOutcomes.kt:291-292), so the save rate is higher for listeners who use playlists than for listeners who use likes, purely because of recording policy. The metric's known-limits KDoc (:218-220) does not mention the split.
- Fix sketch: Pick one rule and document it at ListenerOutcomes.kt:200-220: record every explicit save while paused (an explicit vote is not listening history), or none. Changing SaveWatcher.kt:91 and :113 is the smaller edit and matches the playlist rule.

### [MINOR] Attribution can be lost silently, indistinguishable from an untagged surface

- Evidence: tagServed gives up on a timeout and only logs (TurtleRuntime.kt:411-421); pendingTags are capped at 500 and pruned after a 10 s grace, and pruning runs only while a controller samples (PlayerObserver.kt:230-231, :387-401, :424-431, :454, :457).
- Why it matters: A play that should carry a radio or Infinity tag can arrive untagged under lock contention or a slow page, and the report puts it in the same bucket as an album play. With no live controller tags are never pruned, so the stale-tag problem persists exactly where classification is already weakest.
- Fix sketch: Record the failure instead of only logging it: write a distinct serving path (for example `lost`) when tagAll times out at TurtleRuntime.kt:411-421, and add a counter to the report so untagged-by-failure is separable from untagged-by-surface. Prune tags on a timer rather than only inside sample() (PlayerObserver.kt:387-401).

## Strengths

- **Every rate ships with the denominator that would make it a lie** — FirstFiveCounts.unknownRate (ListenerOutcomes.kt:43-45), untagged share (:51-55, :65), unattributedFirstPlays (:161-174), pending excluded from every rate (:286-288), untagged line at OriginReport.kt:44 | beats Spotify: The numbers reach the reviewer with their own uncertainty attached and the KDoc lists the known limits (ListenerOutcomes.kt:94-95, :218-220). A hosted recommender's internal metrics are not inspectable at all.
- **Measurement is on-device by construction: no telemetry, no impression beacons, no server** — Reports are pure functions over the local store with an injected Clock (ListenerOutcomes.kt:18-23, OriginReport.kt:101-105); the probe reads a copy of turtle.db (LocalOriginReportProbe.kt:12-19); DriveLog never leaves the device (DriveLog.kt:13-17); Sentry defaults off (Settings.kt:612) | beats Spotify: Recommendation quality is measured without collecting a behavioural profile off-device, so evaluation costs the listener no privacy. turtle/AGENTS.md:53 makes it a rule: history stays on device, anything sent off-device is opt-in.
- **A real within-subject experiment arm is already running, with the arm recorded on the play** — SongOrder.ALTERNATE is the default (HomeRows.kt:59-60), flipping per row per local day (TasteEngine.kt:711-721); the arm is written into the tag as ranked or provider (Candidates.kt:61-71, TurtleModule.kt:410-423) and grouped by `served` in both reports | beats Spotify: Daily flipping balances day-of-week effects with no server-side assignment, and the listener's own history is the readout. Spotify's published discovery-satisfaction work relies on panels and hosted experiments; this runs entirely on one device.
- **History corrections are read-time only and reversible, and the listener can see and undo them** — HistoryLens.kt:6-20 (turning an option off restores the previous reading exactly), :34 (copy, never write), :66-86; reset and undo at TurtleRuntime.kt:669-679; both surfaced with a count at ShellSettingsTurtle.kt:375-382, :452-467 | beats Spotify: A listener can tell the recommender to stop counting its own mislabelled skips, or to forget everything it learned, and change their mind, without the data being destroyed. No comparable control exists in a hosted player.
- **The quality gate is wired to every pull request and is a hard merge blocker** — .github/workflows/turtle-engines.yml:3-6, :38-39 runs `./gradlew -p turtle/engines check` on pull_request and on main; OfflineReplayTest.kt:80-88 and GoldenQuerySetTest.kt:118-119 fail the build on a >2% relative regression | beats Spotify: The policy is written in turtle/AGENTS.md:43-45 and enforced by CI rather than by convention, and the baseline values live in version control where a change to them shows up in a diff.
- **The reports are pure, clock-injected and order-independent, so a reviewer can reproduce them exactly** — rateAll sorts on a fixed key (EarlyRuns.kt:19-32); saves merged by a documented tiebreak (ListenerOutcomes.kt:244-245, :252-253); OriginReportTest.kt:186-191 and FirstFiveSkipsTest.kt:188-200 assert shuffled input gives the same report and the same rendered text | beats Spotify: Two people with the same database get byte-identical reports, which is the precondition for arguing about a number instead of about the tool.
- **Discovery is scored by what the listener kept, not by what was served** — DiscoveryReturn pairs a first audible play with a later save or a deliberate replay inside a closed 7-day window (ListenerOutcomes.kt:200-220, :290-303); a surface serving the song again on its own explicitly does not count (:214-215) | beats Spotify: The metric cannot be improved by serving a song more often, the usual failure of play-count-based recommendation metrics. It matches the familiarity-versus-discovery framing in Spotify's own published research rather than raw streams.
- **The measurement's save signal is deliberately kept out of the ranker it measures** — TrackFeedbackKind.SAVED is recorded by SaveWatcher for measurement only (SaveWatcher.kt:25-28) and explicitly ignored by both the profile and the engine (TasteProfile.kt:379, :397; TasteEngine.kt:248, :261) | beats Spotify: The engine cannot learn to optimise its own scorecard through that channel, a discipline most recommender stacks do not keep.
- **Stored plays carry a labelling revision, so a change in how skips are judged is auditable** — PlayEvent.labelVersion with LABEL_VERSION = 2, documented as "a track change the player could not classify is UNKNOWN, never a skip" (PlayEvent.kt:87-91, :113-117); written at ListeningSessionTracker.kt:303; probe filter at LocalOriginReportProbe.kt:34-36 | beats Spotify: The definition of a skip is versioned in the data, so an old number and a new number can be told apart instead of being silently averaged.
- **Negative findings are written down, including the ones that make the product look bad** — 2026-11-23-discovery-first-review.md records 80 of 128 quick skips as false, an Infinity early-skip rate of 16 of 23, 70% untagged plays, and "Treat these figures as a starting point, not a finding"; the KDoc carries the same limits (ListenerOutcomes.kt:94-95, :218-220) | beats Spotify: The failure modes of the measurement are documented beside the measurement, which is what lets an external reviewer check the claim at all.
